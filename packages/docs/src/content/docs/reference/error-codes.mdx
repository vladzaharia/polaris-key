Wire-v3 errors are nested — `{"error":{"code":…}}` — and the not-found body is ONE
shape for "no such product", "service not enabled", and "no such route" (hide-don't-reveal).
17 protocol codes; the worker enum maps each to its response site.

Every code, wire, client and backend, is registered in `conformance/parity/errors.json`
(208 codes), and `pnpm gen constants` generates each SDK's `ErrorCode` constants
from it. A new code needs an entry there first.

## Backend codes (`conformance/parity/errors.json`)

Answered by an SDK's server core when an app calls its own backend with `X-PKey-License` (WIRE-CONTRACT-V4 §14), never by the Worker. The body is `application/problem+json`, `Cache-Control: no-store`, and its `type` is this page with the code as the fragment. A 401 also carries `WWW-Authenticate: PKey-License realm="<product>", error="<code>"`.

### `license_required`

401 from a product backend: the request carries no `X-PKey-License` (WIRE-CONTRACT-V4 §14.2 step 1). The client half shows its gate. Service: `license`. Help: [Help](/docs/help/messages/license-key/#license_required).

### `license_invalid`

401 from a product backend: the `X-PKey-License` value is longer than 16 384 bytes, is not three base64url segments, or fails the strict verifier, its issuer, its product, its device or its `issuedAt` (WIRE-CONTRACT-V4 §14.2 steps 2 and 3). The client half syncs and retries once. Service: `license`. Help: [Help](/docs/help/messages/license-status/#license_invalid).

### `license_stale`

401 from a product backend: the licence document is past its freshness window (`expiresAt`, or the backend's `maxAgeSeconds`, plus 300 s) or the gate finds it unusable (WIRE-CONTRACT-V4 §14.2 steps 4 and 5). The client half syncs and retries once. Service: `license`. Help: [Help](/docs/help/messages/license-status/#license_stale).

### `sign_in_required`

403 from a product backend: the route needs a signed-in account and the licence document names none (`profile.user.subject` absent or malformed; WIRE-CONTRACT-V4 §14.2 step 6). Never retried; the client half offers sign-in. Service: `identity`. Help: [Help](/docs/help/messages/signing-in/#sign_in_required).

### `not_entitled`

403 from a product backend: the route needs an entitlement the licence document does not grant as `true` (WIRE-CONTRACT-V4 §14.2 step 6). The existing wire code, reused. Never retried; the client half surfaces it with the upgrade link, if any. Service: `license`. Help: [Help](/docs/help/messages/license-status/#not_entitled).
