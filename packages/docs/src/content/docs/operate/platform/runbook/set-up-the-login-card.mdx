**Role required:** Platform admin with Cloudflare access. Provider consoles and secrets are the owner's.

**Impact:** A provider appears on every product's login card once all its values are set. Setting the Turnstile secret before the portal renders the widget makes email sign-in fail with `403 turnstile_failed`.

Use this when you add or rotate a sign-in provider, or change the card's checks.

## Verify

- Set the providers on staging first, then sign in with each one in a private window before you repeat it on production.
- A provider appears only once all its values are set and its sealed secret opens. Until then `/login/<provider>` answers that sign-in is not available, and the rest of the card works.
- A sign-in that answers that a provider is unavailable right now means the provider's endpoint failed or answered from a host outside the allowlist.

## Roll back

- Turn a provider off with `wrangler secret delete` on its client id (or Steam's key). People who signed in with it keep their account and use another method.
- A secret sealed under another environment's KEK, or for another provider's slot, does not open, and that provider stays off.

## Escalate

Rotating `PLATFORM_KEK` also means re-sealing these secrets. `legacy.workerSecrets` in `/manage/api/products/kek` names any still under a legacy key; see [Rotate the KEK](/docs/operate/platform/runbook/rotate-the-kek/).
