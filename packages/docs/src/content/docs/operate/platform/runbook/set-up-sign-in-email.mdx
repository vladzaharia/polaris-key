**Role required:** Platform admin with Cloudflare access. DNS and Apple private relay setup is the owner's.

**Impact:** Every product's sign-in email leaves one shared sender, so its reputation is shared. A wrong sender setting stops email sign-in for everyone.

Use this when you set up the sending domain, cap a product's daily email, or clear a suppressed address.

<RunbookSection file="RUNBOOK" heading="Sign-in email" />

## Verify

- Send a sign-in to a test Apple account's private relay address and confirm it arrives. Apple must show each sending domain as SPF-verified.
- Watch Cloudflare's Email Sending analytics: delivery rate over 95 %, hard bounces under 2 %, complaints under 0.1 %.
- A product that hit its cap shows users "We can't send email right now". Raise or reset its cap in the product's **Settings** and the message stops within the sliding 24 hours.

## Roll back

- The Worker sends from `noreply@plrs.im` while `EMAIL_SENDER_ADDRESS` is unset.
- Reset a product's cap in its **Settings** to return it to the deployment value.
- Clear a suppressed address in Cloudflare's list too (**Email Sending**, **Suppressions**). Otherwise the next send adds it back.

## Escalate

Throttling, quota, an unverified sender and provider outages all answer `email_unavailable`, and the login card offers another sign-in method. See [Email delivery](/docs/operate/platform/email/) for the limits and what people see.
