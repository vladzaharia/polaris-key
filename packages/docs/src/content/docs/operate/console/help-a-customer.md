**Requires:** access to **Users**. **Impact:** an export contains personal data, and deleting data can't be undone.

The person's own side of these tasks is in [Manage your data](/docs/help/manage-your-data/) and [Move to a new computer](/docs/help/new-computer/).

## Export a person's data

1. Open **Users**, then the person's record.
2. Select **Export JSON**.
3. Save the file securely.

The file holds only what your product has for that person. The export is recorded in **Activity**.

## Delete a person's data

1. Open **Users**, then the person's record.
2. Select **More actions**, then **Delete data for this product…**.
3. Type `delete`, then select **Delete data**.

This empties the product's data for the person. Their license and Polaris Locker account stay. You can't delete the person's account.

## Move a license to another person

The person who is to hold the license opens your product's page in Polaris Locker (polaris.locker) and selects **Get a support code**. They read the code, like `K7M-4QX-9TZ`, to you.

1. Open **Users**, then the record of the person who holds the license.
2. On the **Licenses** tab, select **Relink…** on the license.
3. Under **Move to user**, enter the support code. Dashes are optional.
4. Give a reason.
5. Select **Relink license**. Sign in again if asked.

Both people get an email before the license moves. To reverse it within 72 hours, open the **Licenses** tab, select **Undo…** on the relink, then **Undo relink**.

The safeguards, **Make floating** and **Reassign** are on [Users](/docs/operate/console/users/#relink-a-license).

## Someone lost every way into their account

Polaris Key can't open a person's account or recover it. When no sign-in method is left, move their license to a new account:

1. Ask the person to make a new Polaris Locker account and tell you its email address.
2. Check that the license is theirs, on your own evidence: a receipt, the buyer email or a store order.
3. Open **License → Licenses**, then the license.
4. Select **Reassign…**. Enter the new email and a reason, and type the license's name to confirm.
5. Select **Reassign license**. Sign in again if asked.

Both addresses are emailed first. The license joins the new account once that address is verified there, and **Undo** works for 72 hours. If the person can read you a support code from your product's page in the new account, **Relink…** moves the license the same way.

Why there is no recovery desk: [Accounts and recovery](/docs/features/sign-in/accounts/#recovery).
