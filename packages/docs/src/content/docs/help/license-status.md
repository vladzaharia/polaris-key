Your license status tells you whether the app can run on a device right now. The app shows it in its
own words, and your app may look different. Your library shows a label only when something needs
attention.

## Active

The license works on this device. Some apps say **License active**. Your library shows no label for
an active license. When the license ends within 14 days, your library shows **Expires in** and a
number of days, or **Expires tomorrow**.

## Offline

The app could not reach the service that checks your license. It keeps working for a limited time
and shows how many days are left, or the date to **Reconnect by**. Connect to the internet and
select **Reconnect**. Some apps call this **Offline grace**. How long it lasts is shown as
**Works offline for** on the license in your library.

## License expired

The license's end date has passed, or the app was offline longer than the license allows. Your
library shows **Expired** and the end date. Connect to the internet and try again. If the app still
says **License expired**, select **Renew** to renew it with the app's developer, or select
**Use another license**.

## Suspended

The app's developer turned the license off. Your library shows **Suspended**, and your devices can
show **Signed out**. Contact the developer to find out why.

## Signed out

The device was removed from the license, or the license was turned off. The app asks you to sign in
or activate again. If you did not remove the device, check **Devices** on the app's page in your
library. See [Manage your devices](/docs/help/devices/).

## Update required

This version of the app is no longer supported for your license. Update the app, then open it
again. **Covers versions** on the license in your library shows which versions your license covers.
See [Updates](/docs/help/update/).

## Device limit reached

Your library shows **Device limit reached** when the license is on every device it allows. See
[Device limit reached](/docs/help/device-limit/).

## Other messages

An app can show other license messages, such as **Not available on this license** or
**Channel not included**. See [All messages](/docs/help/messages/).
