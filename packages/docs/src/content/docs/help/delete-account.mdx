Delete your account to erase your Polaris Key account and the email addresses on it.

<Callout type="danger" title="Deleting your account can't be undone">
  It doesn't cancel or refund anything you bought. Ask the developer or the
  store about that.
</Callout>

## What happens when you delete your account

- Your account is erased with its email addresses, sign-in methods, passkeys, name, picture and
  birth date. Polaris Key signs you out.
- Apps you added from the **Discover** page that need no license leave your library.
- Your licenses stay with each developer. They leave your account, and you can add them again.
- Each developer's records show that your account was deleted, and which of its licenses you held.
  A developer isn't notified. Developers keep their own records of those licenses, including any
  name or email they stored.
- Backups can keep a deleted account's data for up to 30 days.
- Polaris Key emails every verified address on your account before it deletes the account.

## Before you start

Sign in again right before you delete. Polaris Key needs a sign-in from the last 5 minutes. If
yours is older, the page says **Sign in again to continue**, and nothing is deleted. Sign out,
sign in, then try again.

## Steps

1. Open the account menu and select **Account**.
2. Go to **Your data**.
3. Select **Delete account**.
4. Read **Delete your Polaris Key account?**
5. In the box, type your email address. The label shows the address to type.
6. Select **Delete my account**. It stays unavailable until what you typed matches your email.

To stop, select **Keep my account**.

## Check it worked

The page says **Your account was deleted** and **We sent a confirmation to your email.** You are
signed out. The email's subject is **Your Polaris Key account has been deleted**. See
[Emails from Polaris Key](/docs/help/emails/#account-deleted).

## After you delete it

Signing in with the same email creates a new, empty account. Licenses bought with that email can
join it. Add other licenses again with their keys. To ask a developer to erase its own records, see
[Your data and privacy](/docs/help/your-data/).

## If you didn't delete your account

If you got an email with the subject **Your Polaris Key account has been deleted** and you didn't
delete your account, someone may have signed in to your account or to your email. Anyone who can
read your email can sign in with it. If you think someone can, secure your email account first.

The account can't be brought back. Signing in with the same email creates a new account, and
licenses stay with each developer, so you can add them to it. To check that the email is real, see
[Emails from Polaris Key](/docs/help/emails/#account-deleted).

Polaris Key doesn't publish a help address. [Who to contact](/docs/help/contact/) says who to ask.

## Still need help?

If you can't delete your account, see [Who to contact](/docs/help/contact/).
