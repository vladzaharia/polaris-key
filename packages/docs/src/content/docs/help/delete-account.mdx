Delete your account to schedule it for erasure. After 14 days, Polaris Locker erases your account and
its email addresses. You can cancel before then.

<Callout
  type="danger"
  title="After 14 days, deleting your account can't be undone"
>
  It doesn't cancel or refund anything you bought. Ask the developer or the
  store about that.
</Callout>

## What happens when you delete your account

- For 14 days your account stays active. You stay signed in, your licenses and devices keep
  working, and you can download your data or cancel.
- Then your account is erased with its email addresses, sign-in methods, passkeys, name, picture
  and birth date. Locker signs you out.
- Apps you added from the **Discover** page that need no license leave your library.
- Your licenses stay with each developer. They leave your account, and you can add them again.
- Each developer's records show that your account was deleted, and which of its licenses you held.
  A developer isn't notified. Developers keep their own records of those licenses, including any
  name or email they stored.
- Locker emails every verified address on your account when you schedule the deletion, and
  again when the account is erased.
- While a deletion is scheduled, you can't join another account to this one. Cancel it first.

## Before you start

Locker needs a sign-in from the last 5 minutes. If yours is older, the page shows **Confirm it's you first** where the step would be. Confirm with your passkey or an emailed code, and the step runs.

## Steps

1. Open the account menu and select **Account**.
2. Go to **Your data**.
3. Select **Delete account…**.
4. Read **Schedule your Polaris Locker account for deletion?**
5. In the box, type your email address. The label shows the address to type.
6. Select **Schedule deletion**. It stays unavailable until what you typed matches your email.

To stop before you schedule it, select **Cancel**.

## Check it worked

The page shows the date your account is erased and **Cancel deletion**. The email's subject is **Your Polaris Locker account is scheduled for deletion**.

## Cancel a scheduled deletion

1. Open **Account**, then **Your data**.
2. Select **Cancel deletion**. If the page shows **Confirm it's you first**, confirm and the cancel runs.

The page says **Deletion canceled**, and you get an email with the subject **Your Polaris Locker account deletion was cancelled**. Once the erasure has begun, it can't be cancelled.

## After your account is erased

The email's subject is **Your Polaris Locker account has been deleted**. See
[Emails from Polaris Locker](/docs/help/emails/#account-deleted). Signing in with the same email
creates a new, empty account. Licenses bought with that email can join it. To ask a developer to erase its own records, see
[Your data and privacy](/docs/help/your-data/).

## If you didn't delete your account

If you got an email with the subject **Your Polaris Locker account is scheduled for deletion** and you
didn't ask for it, someone may have signed in to your account or to your email. Secure your email
account first, then sign in to Locker and select **Cancel deletion**. See
[Secure your account](/docs/help/secure-your-account/).

If you got the email **Your Polaris Locker account has been deleted** and didn't ask for it, the
account can't be brought back. Sign in with the same email to create a new account.
