Use this page when an email from Polaris Key asks **Wasn't you?** and you don't recognize the
change. Work through the steps in order.

<Callout type="caution" title="Important">
  Anyone who can read your email can ask for a sign-in code. If you think
  someone has access to your email, Google, Apple or Steam account, secure that
  account first. Otherwise the steps below don't keep them out.
</Callout>

## Open your sign-in methods

In the email, select **Secure your account**. It opens **Sign-in methods** in your account. The
link only opens the page and doesn't sign anyone in, so sign in first if the page asks.

If you're not sure the email is real, don't select the link. Go to
[key.plrs.im](https://key.plrs.im) yourself and sign in if it asks. Then open the account menu and
select **Sign-in methods**. To tell a real email from a fake one, read
[Emails from Polaris Key](/docs/help/emails/).

## Remove sign-in methods you don't recognize

Do this first. A method you didn't add lets someone sign in again.

1. Under **Sign-in methods**, find each email, account or passkey you didn't add.
2. Select the button next to it: **Remove** for an email or passkey, **Disconnect** for Apple,
   Google or Steam.
3. If you see **Confirm it's you first**, confirm it's you. A passkey or an emailed code removes the
   method at once. See [Sign in to Polaris Key](/docs/help/sign-in/#step_up_required).
4. If you don't see that prompt, or after **Sign in again with**, select the button that names the
   method and starts with **Remove** or **Disconnect**.

## Sign out browsers you don't recognize

1. Under **Where you're signed in**, select **Sign out** next to each browser you don't recognize.
2. If you're not sure which browser is yours, select **Sign out everywhere**.
3. Select **Sign out everywhere** again to confirm.

**Sign out everywhere** does this:

- It signs out every browser, this one included.
- Apps you signed in to with Polaris Key sign out too. A device that got its license by signing in
  stops using it.
- Your licenses stay in your library.

Sign in again afterwards.

## Remove devices you don't recognize

Open each app in your library, find **Devices**, and select **Remove** next to a device you don't
recognize. See [Devices](/docs/help/devices/).

## Optional: add a passkey

Add a passkey, and you can confirm it's you with it later instead of an emailed code. See
[Use a passkey](/docs/help/passkeys/).

## Check it worked

Only sign-in methods and browsers you recognize are left. Each removed method sends a notice to
every verified address on your account.

## Still need help?

- If the sign-in code doesn't arrive, read
  [Your sign-in code didn't arrive](/docs/help/code-didnt-arrive/).
- If someone else changed your email or sign-in methods and you can't sign in, see
  [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/). Polaris Key doesn't publish a help address.
