Use this page when an email from Polaris Key asks **Wasn't you?** and you don't recognize the
change. Work through the steps in order.

<Callout
  type="caution"
  title="Anyone who can read your email can ask for a sign-in code"
>
  If you think someone has access to your email, Google, Apple or Steam account,
  secure that account first. Otherwise the steps below don't keep them out.
</Callout>

## Open your sign-in methods

In the email, select **Secure your account**. It opens **Sign-in methods** in your account. The
link only opens the page and doesn't sign anyone in, so sign in first if the page asks.

## Remove sign-in methods you don't recognize

Do this first. A method you didn't add lets someone sign in again.

1. Under **Sign-in methods**, find each email, account or passkey you didn't add.
2. Select **Remove** next to an email or passkey. Select **Disconnect** next to Apple, Google or
   Steam.
3. If you see **Confirm it's you first**, confirm it's you. See
   [Sign in to Polaris Key](/docs/help/sign-in/#step_up_required). Otherwise select the button
   that names the method and starts with **Remove** or **Disconnect**.

## Sign out where you don't recognize

Under **Where you're signed in**, select **Sign out** next to each browser you don't recognize. If
you're not sure which is yours, select **Sign out everywhere**, then **Sign out everywhere** again
to confirm. That signs out every browser, this one included, and the apps you signed in to with
Polaris Key. A device that got its license by signing in stops using it. Your licenses stay in
your library. Sign in again afterwards.

## Remove devices you don't recognize

Open each app in your library, find **Devices**, and select **Remove** next to a device you don't
recognize. See [Devices](/docs/help/devices/).

## Optional: add a passkey

Add a passkey, and you can confirm it's you with it later instead of an emailed code. See
[Use a passkey](/docs/help/passkeys/).

## Check it worked

Only sign-in methods and browsers you recognize are left. Each removed method sends a notice to
every verified address on your account.

## Still need help?

If you can't sign in, read [Your sign-in code didn't arrive](/docs/help/code-didnt-arrive/). For
anything else, read [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/).
