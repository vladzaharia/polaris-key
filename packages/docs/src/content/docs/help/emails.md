Account emails come from Polaris Locker, at `noreply@auth.plrs.im` or `noreply@plrs.im`. None asks for your full license key.

Ignore a code or link you didn't ask for; nothing changes until it is used. Code didn't arrive? See [Your sign-in code didn't arrive](/docs/help/code-didnt-arrive/).

## Your library

### <a id="license-key"></a>Your <var>App</var> license

Enter the key in the app. Keep it private.

### <a id="license-invited"></a><var>App</var> license is ready

Sign in with this address. No key.

### <a id="license-added"></a><var>App</var> is in your library

Select **Open**, then the app's name.

### <a id="license-from-developer"></a><var>App</var> is in your Polaris Locker library

Select **Open**, then the app's name.

### <a id="license-attached"></a>Your <var>App</var> license was added to a Polaris Locker account

Added with its key. Not you? Contact the app's developer.

### <a id="license-key-replaced"></a>Your license has a new key

The old key can't activate new devices; existing ones keep working.

### <a id="download-link"></a>Download <var>App</var> for <var>System</var>

Open the link on the device you're installing on.

## Devices and sign-in

### <a id="device-removed"></a><var>Device</var> was removed from <var>App</var>

Choose **See your devices** to review it.

### <a id="device-replaced"></a><var>App</var> signed out on <var>Device</var>

Another device replaced this one. Choose **Open Polaris Locker**.

### <a id="app-device-approved"></a><var>App</var> signed in on <var>Device</var>

You approved it from another device. If it wasn't you, choose **See your devices** and remove it.

### <a id="new-device-sign-in"></a>A new device signed in to your Polaris Locker account

Choose **Open Polaris Locker** to review.

### <a id="recovery-started"></a>Recovery started on <var>Device</var>

A device is unlocking your synced data with the recovery code. Not you? Choose **See your devices** and remove it. No one asks for this code.

### <a id="recovery-code-changed"></a>Your <var>App</var> recovery code changed

The old code no longer works. Not you? [Secure your account](/docs/help/secure-your-account/).

### <a id="encryption-reset"></a><var>App</var> encryption was reset

New key and recovery code. A device with the old key can move your data over for 30 days. Not you? [Secure your account](/docs/help/secure-your-account/).

### <a id="packages-token"></a>A personal token was created for your Polaris Locker account

A command line got read-only access to your packages. Revoke it under **Packages**.

### <a id="sign-in-method-added"></a><var>Method</var> was connected to your Polaris Locker account

Not you? [Secure your account](/docs/help/secure-your-account/).

### <a id="sign-in-method-removed"></a><var>Method</var> was disconnected from your Polaris Locker account

Not you? [Secure your account](/docs/help/secure-your-account/).

### <a id="primary-email-changed"></a>Your Polaris Locker primary email was changed

Security emails now go to another verified email. Not you? [Secure your account](/docs/help/secure-your-account/).

## Your account

### <a id="account-deletion-scheduled"></a>Your Polaris Locker account is scheduled for deletion

Choose **Review account settings** to cancel before the erase date. See [Delete your account](/docs/help/delete-account/).

### <a id="account-deletion-cancelled"></a>Your Polaris Locker account deletion was canceled

Nothing is erased.

### <a id="account-deleted"></a>Your Polaris Locker account has been deleted

Your account, email addresses and sign-in methods were erased. Your licenses stay with each developer; sign in to add them back.

### <a id="accounts-merged"></a>Two Polaris Locker accounts were joined

Not you? [Secure your account](/docs/help/secure-your-account/).

### <a id="accounts-separated"></a>Your Polaris Locker accounts were separated

Not you? [Secure your account](/docs/help/secure-your-account/).

## License changes

If a change is unexpected, contact the developer's support within 72 hours; they can undo it.

### <a id="license-link-superseded"></a>A <var>App</var> license left your library

Another account holds it now. Bought it? Ask the developer to move it.

### <a id="license-relinked-away"></a>The developer moved your <var>App</var> license to another account

It now belongs to the other account.

### <a id="license-relinked-in"></a><var>App</var> is being added to your library

Select **Open**, then the app's name.

### <a id="license-relink-undone"></a>The developer undid a move of a <var>App</var> license

The license returns to the original account.

### <a id="license-made-floating"></a>The developer removed a <var>App</var> license from your account

Also sent as **A <var>App</var> license is no longer assigned to you**, when your email address was removed from a waiting license.

### <a id="license-reassigned-away"></a>The developer assigned your <var>App</var> license to someone else

Someone else holds it now.

### <a id="license-assigned-to-you"></a>A <var>App</var> license was assigned to you

Sign in with that email, then select **Open** and the app's name.
