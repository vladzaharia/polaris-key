Account emails come from **Polaris Key**, sent from `noreply@auth.plrs.im` or `noreply@plrs.im`.
Neither is a help address. None asks for your full license key.

A sign-in or confirmation email has a code that works once for 10 minutes. If it does not arrive,
check your spam folder, then select **Send a new code**; it replaces the old one. If you did not ask
for an email, leave it unused.

**Open** and **Download** buttons end with the app's name.

In an unexpected security notice, select **Secure your account** to open your sign-in methods.
Notice links do not sign anyone in.

## Your library

<h3 id="license-added">A license was added</h3>

A license for an app was added to your library. Select **Open** to see it.

<h3 id="license-attached">A license was attached</h3>

A license was added with its key to a Polaris Key account. If this was not you, contact the app's
developer.

<h3 id="license-key-replaced">Your license has a new key</h3>

A license has a new key. Select **Open** to view it. The old key cannot activate new devices;
existing devices keep working. If unexpected, contact the developer.

<h3 id="download-link">Your download link</h3>

On the device you use to install the app, select **Download** to open its download page in your
library. You may need to sign in first.

## Devices and sign-in

<h3 id="device-removed">A device was removed</h3>

A device was removed from an app. Choose **See your devices** to review it, or **Secure your
account** if it was not you.

<h3 id="new-device-sign-in">A new device signed in</h3>

A new device signed in to your Polaris Key account. Use **Open Polaris Key** to see where you are
signed in, or **Secure your account** if it was not you.

<h3 id="sign-in-method-added">A sign-in method was added</h3>

A sign-in method was connected to your Polaris Key account. Choose **Secure your account** if you
did not add it.

<h3 id="sign-in-method-removed">A sign-in method was removed</h3>

A sign-in method was disconnected from your Polaris Key account. Choose **Secure your account** if
you did not remove it.

## Your account

<h3 id="account-deleted">Your account was deleted</h3>

Your account, its email addresses, and its sign-in methods were erased as you asked. This cannot be
undone. If you did not ask for this, sign in again to create a new account. Your licenses stay with
each developer, so you can add them back. To ask a developer to erase its own records, contact its
support.

<h3 id="accounts-merged">Accounts were joined</h3>

Two Polaris Key accounts were joined after someone signed in to both. Their sign-in methods and
licenses are now together. If this was not you, choose **Secure your account**.

<h3 id="accounts-separated">Accounts were separated</h3>

Two joined accounts are separate again, each with its own sign-in methods and licenses. If this was
not you, choose **Secure your account**.

## License changes

<h3 id="license-link-superseded">A license link was replaced</h3>

The license now belongs to another account and left your library. If you bought it, ask the
developer to move it.

<h3 id="license-relinked-away">A license moved to another account</h3>

The developer moved a license to another account. If unexpected, contact the developer's support
within 72 hours; they can undo it.

<h3 id="license-relinked-in">A license moved to your account</h3>

An app license is being added to your library. Select **Open** to see it. If unexpected, contact
the developer's support within 72 hours.

<h3 id="license-relink-undone">A license move was undone</h3>

The developer undid an earlier license move. It returns to the original account. If this was
unexpected, contact the developer's support.

<h3 id="license-made-floating">A license was removed or is no longer assigned</h3>

The developer removed a license from your account, or removed your email address from a license
waiting for you. If unexpected, contact the developer's support within 72 hours; they can undo it.

<h3 id="license-reassigned-away">A license was reassigned</h3>

The developer assigned a license to someone else. It leaves your library, or no longer belongs to
your email address. If unexpected, contact the developer's support within 72 hours; they can undo
it.

<h3 id="license-assigned-to-you">A license was assigned to you</h3>

An app license was assigned to your email address. Sign in with that email, then select **Open** to
find it in your library.
