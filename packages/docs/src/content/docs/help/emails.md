Account emails come from Polaris Locker, sent from `noreply@auth.plrs.im` or `noreply@plrs.im`.
None asks for your full license key.

If you did not ask for an email, leave it unused. A sign-in code that does not arrive: see [Your
sign-in code didn't arrive](/docs/help/code-didnt-arrive/).

In an unexpected security notice, select **Secure your account** to open your sign-in methods.

## Your library

### <a id="license-added"></a><var>App</var> is in your library

A license was added to your library. Select **Open** and the app's name to see it.

### <a id="license-attached"></a>Your <var>App</var> license was added to a Polaris Locker account

A license was added with its key. If this was not you, contact the app's developer.

### <a id="license-key-replaced"></a>Your license has a new key

The old key cannot activate new devices; existing devices keep working. If unexpected, contact the developer.

### <a id="download-link"></a>Download <var>App</var> for <var>System</var>

Select **Download** and the app's name on the device you install on. You may need to sign in first.

## Devices and sign-in

### <a id="device-removed"></a><var>Device</var> was removed from <var>App</var>

Choose **See your devices** to review it. Choose **Secure your account** if it was not you.

### <a id="new-device-sign-in"></a>A new device signed in to your Polaris Locker account

Use **Open Polaris Locker** to see where you are signed in. Choose **Secure your account** if it was not you.

### <a id="sign-in-method-added"></a><var>Method</var> was connected to your Polaris Locker account

Choose **Secure your account** if it was not you.

### <a id="sign-in-method-removed"></a><var>Method</var> was disconnected from your Polaris Locker account

Choose **Secure your account** if it was not you.

### <a id="primary-email-changed"></a>Your Polaris Locker primary email was changed

Another verified email is now the primary. Security notices go there. Choose **See your sign-in methods**. Choose **Secure your account** if it was not you.

## Your account

### <a id="account-deletion-scheduled"></a>Your Polaris Locker account is scheduled for deletion

The email gives the erase date. Choose **Review account settings** to cancel before then. See [Delete your account](/docs/help/delete-account/).

### <a id="account-deletion-cancelled"></a>Your Polaris Locker account deletion was cancelled

Nothing is erased.

### <a id="account-deleted"></a>Your Polaris Locker account has been deleted

Your account, its email addresses and its sign-in methods were erased as you asked. If you did not ask for this, sign in to create a new account. Your licenses stay with each developer, so you can add them back.

### <a id="accounts-merged"></a>Two Polaris Locker accounts were joined

Their sign-in methods and licenses are now together. Choose **Secure your account** if it was not you.

### <a id="accounts-separated"></a>Your Polaris Locker accounts were separated

Two joined accounts are separate again. Choose **Secure your account** if it was not you.

## License changes

### <a id="license-link-superseded"></a>A <var>App</var> license left your library

The license now belongs to another account. If you bought it, ask the developer to move it.

### <a id="license-relinked-away"></a>The developer moved your <var>App</var> license to another account

If unexpected, contact the developer's support within 72 hours; they can undo it.

### <a id="license-relinked-in"></a><var>App</var> is being added to your library

Select **Open** and the app's name to see it. If unexpected, contact the developer's support within 72 hours; they can undo it.

### <a id="license-relink-undone"></a>The developer undid a move of a <var>App</var> license

The license returns to the original account. If unexpected, contact the developer's support.

### <a id="license-made-floating"></a>The developer removed a <var>App</var> license from your account

Also sent as **A <var>App</var> license is no longer assigned to you**, when your email address was removed from a waiting license. If unexpected, contact the developer's support within 72 hours; they can undo it.

### <a id="license-reassigned-away"></a>The developer assigned your <var>App</var> license to someone else

If unexpected, contact the developer's support within 72 hours; they can undo it.

### <a id="license-assigned-to-you"></a>A <var>App</var> license was assigned to you

Sign in with that email, then select **Open** and the app's name.
