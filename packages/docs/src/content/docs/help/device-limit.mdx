Your license is already on all the devices it allows. To use the app on this device, free one of
the others.

<Callout type="caution" title="Important">
  Polaris Key cannot raise the limit. Only the app's developer can. To ask for
  more devices, see [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/).
</Callout>

## What happens when you free a device

The other device stops counting toward your limit, and its app asks to be activated again. Its saved
files stay on it. You can add it back later. See [Manage your devices](/docs/help/devices/).

## Free a device

Your app may look different. Use the branch that matches what it shows.

### The app lists your devices

1. Choose the device to replace. The app marks the device that has gone longest without use as
   **Least recent**.
2. Read what happens, then select **Replace and continue**.

### The app sends you to your library

If the app shows **Open in browser**, or a code to scan with your phone:

1. Select **Open in browser**, or scan the code, and sign in to Polaris Key.
2. Choose the device to remove, then select **Remove** followed by the device's name.
3. Go back to the app and select **Try again**.

If the license is not in your library yet, the page first asks you to add its key. Add it, then free
a device. See [Add a license with a key](/docs/help/add-a-license/).

### The app shows neither

Free a device yourself. In your library, remove it as described in
[Manage your devices](/docs/help/devices/). To free the computer you are holding, see
[Move your license to a new computer](/docs/help/new-computer/).

## Check it worked

The app activates and shows **Activated**.

## Undo

Activate the device you removed again, as long as you are under the limit.
