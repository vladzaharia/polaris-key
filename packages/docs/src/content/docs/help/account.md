Your Polaris Key account holds your profile and the ways you sign in. Open the account menu and
select **Account**. To leave this browser, select **Sign out** at the top of the page.

## Profile

Select **Edit profile** to change your **Screen name** and **Picture**. They show in Polaris Key,
and in an app once you agree to share them. A birth date is optional. It stays private to you:
apps never receive it.

## Sign-in methods

You need at least one sign-in method.

- **Add an email** sends a 6-digit code to check the address is yours. Licenses bought with a
  verified email can join your library.
- **Connect** adds Apple, Google or Steam, when the page offers them.
- **Add a passkey** adds a passkey. See [Use a passkey](/docs/help/passkeys/).
- **Remove** or **Disconnect** takes a method away. The last method can't be removed.

Adding or removing a method asks you to **Confirm it's you first**. See
[Sign in to Polaris Key](/docs/help/sign-in/#step_up_required). Each change is recorded, and
Polaris Key emails every verified address on your account. To add a method again, use the same
steps. If you didn't make a change, read [Secure your account](/docs/help/secure-your-account/).

## Where you're signed in

This lists the browsers signed in to your account. Yours is marked **This browser**. Select
**Sign out** next to another browser to end it. **Sign out everywhere** ends every browser,
this one included, and signs you out of apps you signed in to with Polaris Key. Devices that use a
license are on each app's page in your library.

## Appearance

Choose a **Theme**: **Match my device**, **Dark** or **Light**. Choose **Motion**: **Match my
device** or **Reduced**, which turns animation off. Your choices apply in this browser only.

## Your data

**Download my data** saves your whole account as one file. **Delete account** schedules your
account to be erased after 14 days, and you can cancel before then. Read
[Delete your account](/docs/help/delete-account/) first. For what apps and Polaris Key hold about
you, read [Your data and privacy](/docs/help/your-data/).
