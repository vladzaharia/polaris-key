If you bought something in a store, the app can ask the store for your purchases again and apply them. Use **Restore purchase** in the app.

:::caution[Important]
**Restore purchase** appears only in apps and stores that support it. It works with the App Store, Google Play and Steam.
:::

## Before you start

Use the store account you bought with. The app can restore only the purchases that store account holds.

## Restore in the app

1. Open the app.
2. On the welcome screen, or on the screen that says to get the app from a store, select **Restore purchase**. Some apps say **Restore purchases**.

On a Mac, buttons use capital letters, such as **Restore Purchase**. For other ways to start using an app, see [Activate your app](/docs/help/activate/).

## If your library doesn't show the app

Your library at [key.plrs.im](https://key.plrs.im) lists an app once a license for it is linked to your account. If you bought in a store and the app is missing, look under **Missing a license?** in your library.

- **Steam:** add Steam to your account.
  1. Sign in at [key.plrs.im](https://key.plrs.im). If you have never signed in, choose any sign-in option the page offers. If you can't sign in, see [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/).
  2. Open the account menu and select **Sign-in methods**.
  3. Under **Accounts**, select **Connect** next to **Steam**. If you see **Disconnect** there instead, Steam is already added and you can stop.
  4. If the page says **Confirm it's you first**, confirm with the method it offers.
  5. Finish signing in with Steam.
- **App Store or Google Play:** sign in once in the app itself, then check your library again.

## If it doesn't work

- If nothing is restored, check that you're signed in to the store with the account you bought with.
- A store purchase links to only one license. Sign in the same way you did the first time. If you used a license key, enter that same key.
- A purchase the store has refunded can't be restored unless the store reverses the refund. See [Refunds, billing and cancellations](/docs/help/refunds/).

## Check it worked

The app unlocks what you bought, or your library lists the app.
