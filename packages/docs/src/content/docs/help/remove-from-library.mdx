Removing an app takes it off your library page. What else it does depends on how the app got there.

## What happens when you remove an app

If you added the app with a license key, its devices keep working. The license leaves your account
and does not come back to it by itself. To add it again, use its key. If no email address is tied to
the license, anyone with the key can add it to their own account.

If you added a free app from **Discover**, nothing else changes. You can add it again from Discover
while its developer offers it.

Removing an app does not free a device. To free one, see
[Manage your devices](/docs/help/devices/).

## Remove an app you added with a key

1. Sign in at [key.plrs.im](https://key.plrs.im) and open the app's page.
2. Select the three-dot menu next to the main button, then **Remove from my library**.
3. Read what happens, then select **Remove from my library**. Select **Keep it** to stop.

If you hold more than one license for the app, first choose the one you want to remove in the
license list on the page. The dialog names that license, and your other licenses stay.

If the library asks you to sign in again, sign in and repeat the steps. Removing a license needs a
sign-in from the last 5 minutes.

## Remove a free app

1. Find the app's tile in your library, or open its page.
2. Select the three-dot menu, then **Remove from library**.
3. Select **Remove from library** again. Select **Keep it** to stop.

## If there is no remove option

The menu has no remove option when the license has no key to add it back with, such as a license
the app gave you when you signed in. It also has none when the developer does not allow adding by
key.

## Check it worked

The app is gone from your library, and a message says it was removed.

## Undo

Add the app again. Use **Activate license** with its key, or add a free app from **Discover**. See
[Add a license with a key](/docs/help/add-a-license/) and
[Get free apps from Discover](/docs/help/discover/).
