Removing an app takes it off your library page. What else it does depends on how the app got there.

<Callout type="caution" title="Important">
  Before you remove an app you added with a key, make sure you still have the
  key. Without it you cannot add the app back. If you lost the key, see [Find a
  lost license key](/docs/help/lost-key/).
</Callout>

## What happens when you remove an app

If you added the app with a license key, its devices keep working. The license leaves your account
and does not come back to it by itself. If no email address is tied to the license, anyone with the
key can add it to their own account.

If the app is a free app that needs no license, nothing else changes. You can add it again from
**Discover** while its developer offers it.

Removing an app does not free a device. To free one, see
[Manage your devices](/docs/help/devices/).

## Remove an app you added with a key

1. Sign in at [polaris.locker](https://polaris.locker).
2. Open the app's page.
3. If you hold more than one license for the app, choose the one you want to remove in the license
   list on the page.
4. Open the three-dot menu next to the main button.
5. Select **Remove from my library**.
6. Read what happens. If you hold more than one license, the dialog names the one you chose, and
   your other licenses stay.
7. Select **Remove from my library** to confirm.

To stop, select **Keep it**.

If the dialog says **Sign in again to continue**, sign out from your account menu, sign back in and
repeat the steps. Removing a license needs a sign-in from the last 5 minutes.

## Remove a free app that needs no license

Its tile says **Free to use**.

1. Find the app's tile in your library, or open its page.
2. Open the three-dot menu.
3. Select **Remove from library**.
4. Select **Remove from library** again to confirm.

To stop, select **Keep it**.

## If there is no remove option

The menu has no remove option when the license has no key to add it back with, such as a license
the app gave you when you signed in, or a license you added from **Discover**, for example
**Free with a Polaris Locker account**. It also has none when the developer does not allow adding by
key.

You may see this message if a removal is refused.

<HelpMessage id="not_removable" />

## Check it worked

The app is gone from your library, and a message says it was removed. If you held more than one
license, the app stays and the message says which license was removed.

## Undo

Add the app again. Use **Activate license** with its key. For a free app that needs no license, add
it from **Discover** again. See [Add a license with a key](/docs/help/add-a-license/) and
[Get free apps from Discover](/docs/help/discover/).
