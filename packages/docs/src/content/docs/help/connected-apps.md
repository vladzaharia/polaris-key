Some websites and apps let you **sign in with Polaris Locker**. The first time, a card shows who is asking and what it would get, and you choose. **Connected apps** in your account lists each one you agreed to, so you can look again, change what it gets, or end it.

## What you see

Each app shows its name and developer, when you last used it, and what it gets: your name, your picture, your email address, which features you have, and whether it may keep you signed in while you're away. What you declined is listed too.

A name, a picture and an email address are the same for the app and for the developer's other apps. If a different company runs the app, the card says "by" and that company's name, and says it can tell you're the same person the service's own apps know.

## Change what an app gets

Choose **Change what the app gets** on its row, then switch an optional item on or off and save. The app sees the change the next time it asks, and not before.

## Disconnect an app

Choose **Disconnect** on its row. The app can't read your details anymore, and it can't sign you in without asking you again. A sign-in it already holds can last up to 10 minutes. Disconnecting doesn't delete anything the app stored about you: ask the app's developer for that.

## If you don't see an app

Only apps you signed in to with Polaris Locker are here. Your licenses and devices are in your [library](/docs/help/library/). To remove your account and its connections, see [delete your account](/docs/help/delete-account/).
