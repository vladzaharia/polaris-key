Polaris Key is a service an app's developer can use for licenses, sign-in, and downloads. It is
the name you may see in your browser, in account emails, and at [key.plrs.im](https://key.plrs.im).

Your library at [key.plrs.im](https://key.plrs.im) lists the apps connected to your account. You may
be able to add an app, sign in, or download it there. Some apps use only a license key.

The app's developer makes and supports the app. Polaris Key does not sell apps or issue refunds.

Need help with an app? Read [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/).
