Adding a license key links the app and its license to your Polaris Key account. The app stays in
your library even if you lose the key.

<Callout type="caution" title="Important">
  Adding a key to your library does not activate the app on a device. To use the
  app on this device, activate it in the app. See [Activate your
  app](/docs/help/activate/).
</Callout>

## Before you start

Have the key from your purchase email or from the app's developer. A key starts with `pkey_` and is
case-sensitive. Paste it instead of typing it, because the letters l, 1, O and 0 look alike.

## Add the key

1. Sign in at [key.plrs.im](https://key.plrs.im).
2. Select **Activate license**. On a small screen, the button reads **Activate**.
3. Paste the key into **License key**, then select **Continue**.
4. Check the license the dialog shows, such as how long it lasts and how many devices it allows.
5. Select **Add** followed by the app's name.
6. When the dialog says the app is in your library, select **Open** followed by the app's name, or
   **Activate another**.

If an app or an email sent you here, the dialog opens with a note saying who sent you, and the key
may already be filled in. Check that it matches the key you have.

## Check it worked

The app appears in your library, and the dialog says **In your library**.

## Undo

Remove the app from your library. Its key still works, so you can add it again. See
[Remove an app from your library](/docs/help/remove-from-library/).

## If the key is not accepted

The dialog checks the key before it sends it. It tells you when the key is cut short, too long, has
a space or line break in it, or is not a Polaris Key license key. Copy the whole key again in one
piece. A Steam key is activated in Steam, not here.

If the dialog says it could not find the key, paste the key instead of typing it. If it says there
were too many tries, wait a minute and try again. If it says the developer manages the license
elsewhere, the license cannot be added here, so ask the developer.

<HelpMessage id="license_owned" />

The dialog can offer two ways forward. **Sign in to that account** takes you to sign in with the
account that has the license. **Use a different key** clears the field. If the license should be
yours, ask the app's developer to move it.

<HelpMessage id="email_mismatch" />

The license is tied to an email address that your account has not verified. Select
**Add and verify that email**. It opens **Sign-in methods** with **Add an email** ready. Enter that
address, enter the code Polaris Key sends to it, then add the key again.

<HelpMessage id="key_entry_limit" />

Some apps let a key be entered only a limited number of times. When the limit is used up, add the
key to your account here, then sign in to the app instead of entering the key. The dialog accepts
the key with a warning: select **Add** followed by the app's name.
