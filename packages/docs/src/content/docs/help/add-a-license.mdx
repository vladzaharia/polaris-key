Adding a license key links the app and its license to your Polaris Locker account. The app stays in
your library even if you lose the key.

<Callout type="caution" title="Important">
  Adding a key to your library does not activate the app on a device. To use the
  app on this device, activate it in the app. See [Activate your
  app](/docs/help/activate/).
</Callout>

## Before you start

Have the key from your purchase email or from the app's developer. A key starts with `pkey_` and is
case-sensitive. Paste it instead of typing it, because the lowercase letter L and the number 1 look
alike, and so do the capital letter O and the number 0.

## Add the key

1. Sign in at [polaris.locker](https://polaris.locker).
2. Select **Activate license**. On a small screen the button reads **Activate**. On a phone, it is in
   the middle of the bar at the bottom.
3. Paste the key into **License key**.
4. Select **Continue**.
5. Check the license the dialog shows, such as how long it lasts and how many devices it allows.
6. Select the button that reads **Add** and the app's name.

If an app or an email sent you here, the dialog opens with a note saying who sent you, and the key
may already be filled in. Check that it matches the key you have.

## Check it worked

The dialog says **In your library**, and the app appears in your library. To see the app, select the
button that reads **Open** and the app's name. To add another key, select **Activate another**.

## Undo

Remove the app from your library. Its key still works, so you can add it again. See
[Remove an app from your library](/docs/help/remove-from-library/).

## If the key is not accepted

The dialog checks the key before it sends it. What to do depends on what the dialog says:

- The key is cut short, too long, has a space or line break in it, or is not a Locker license
  key: copy the whole key again in one piece.
- The key looks like a Steam key: activate that key in Steam, not here.
- The dialog could not find the key: paste the key instead of typing it.
- Too many tries: wait a minute, then try again.
- The developer manages the license elsewhere: it cannot be added here, so ask the developer.

You may see this message if the license is in another account.

<HelpMessage id="license_owned" brief />

The dialog offers **Use a different key**, which clears the field. If the license should be yours,
ask the app's developer to move it.

You may see this message if the license is tied to an email your account has not verified.

<HelpMessage id="email_mismatch" brief />

To fix it:

1. Select **Add and verify that email**. It opens **Sign-in methods** with **Add an email** ready.
2. Enter that address.
3. Enter the code Locker sends to it.
4. Add the key again.

If you cannot open that email, ask the app's developer to give the license to an email you can open.

You may see this message if an app limits how often a key can be entered.

<HelpMessage id="key_entry_limit" brief />

When the limit is used up, you can still add the key to your account:

1. Add the key with the steps in [Add the key](#add-the-key). The dialog accepts it with a warning.
2. Sign in to the app instead of entering the key.
