A passkey is a sign-in saved on your device and unlocked the way you unlock the device, such as
with your face or your PIN. In Polaris Key, you use a passkey to confirm it's you before you
change your account.

## Before you start

You need a verified email address on your account, a browser that can make passkeys, and a
sign-in from the last 5 minutes. A passkey made here works only with Polaris Key.

## Add a passkey

1. Open the account menu and select **Sign-in methods**.
2. Under **Passkeys**, select **Add a passkey**.
3. If you see **Confirm it's you first**, confirm it's you, then select **Add a passkey** again.
4. Follow your device's prompt to save the passkey.

If **Add a passkey** is unavailable, the page says why: add an email first, you already have 20
passkeys, or this browser can't make passkeys.

## Check it worked

The passkey appears under **Passkeys**, named for where it was saved, such as **iCloud
Keychain**. Polaris Key emails every verified address on your account. See
[A sign-in method was added](/docs/help/emails/#sign-in-method-added).

## Use your passkey

When Polaris Key asks you to **Confirm it's you first**, select the button that starts with **Use
your passkey to**. Then unlock the passkey. Your browser offers only passkeys saved for this
account.

## Remove a passkey

1. Open **Sign-in methods** and find the passkey under **Passkeys**.
2. Select **Remove** next to it.
3. If you see **Confirm it's you first**, confirm it's you. Otherwise select the button that
   starts with **Remove** and names the passkey. Select **Keep it** to stop.

Removing a passkey doesn't delete it from your device. Delete it there too.

<HelpMessage id="last_link" />

Polaris Key keeps at least one way for you to sign in. The last one shows **Only method**, and
its **Remove** button stays unavailable. Add another sign-in method first, such as an email
address. The same holds for your only email address.

## Still need help?

If a passkey doesn't work, read [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/).
