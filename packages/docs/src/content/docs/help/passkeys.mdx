A passkey is a sign-in saved on your device and unlocked the way you unlock the device, such as
with your face or your PIN. In Polaris Locker, you use a passkey to confirm it's you before you add
or remove a sign-in method. A passkey doesn't replace the email code on the sign-in page, so keep
an email address on your account.

## Before you start

You need a verified email address on your account, a browser that can make passkeys, and a
sign-in from the last 5 minutes. A passkey made here works only with Locker.

## Add a passkey

1. Open the account menu and select **Sign-in methods**.
2. Under **Passkeys**, select **Add a passkey**.
3. If you see **Confirm it's you first**, confirm it's you.
4. Select **Add a passkey** again when the page says **Thanks, that's you. Now add your passkey.**
5. Follow your device's prompt to save the passkey.

If **Add a passkey** is unavailable, the page says why: add an email first, you already have 20
passkeys, or this browser can't make passkeys.

## Check it worked

The passkey appears under **Passkeys**, named for where it was saved, such as **iCloud
Keychain**. Locker emails every verified address on your account. See
[A sign-in method was added](/docs/help/emails/#sign-in-method-added).

## Use your passkey

When Locker asks you to **Confirm it's you first**, select **Use your passkey**. Then unlock
the passkey. Your browser offers only passkeys saved for this account. If your passkey doesn't
work, select **Use an email code instead**, then **Email me a code**.

## Remove a passkey

1. Open **Sign-in methods** and find the passkey under **Passkeys**.
2. Select **Remove** next to it.
3. If you see **Confirm it's you first**, confirm it's you. A passkey or an emailed code removes the
   passkey at once.
4. If you don't see that prompt, or after **Sign in again with**, select the button that starts with
   **Remove** and names the passkey.

To stop, select **Keep it**. Removing a passkey doesn't delete it from your device. Delete it
there too.

The message for a last sign-in method that can't be removed is:

<HelpMessage id="last_link" />

In your account the last method shows **Only method**, and its **Remove** or **Disconnect** button
stays unavailable. For a passkey the page says **This is your only way to sign in. Connect another
one first, then you can remove this passkey.** Add another sign-in method first, such as an email
address. For your only email address the page says **This is your account's only email address.
Add another one first, then you can remove it.**

## Still need help?

If a passkey still doesn't work, see [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/).
