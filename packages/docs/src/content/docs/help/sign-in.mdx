Polaris Key is a service an app's developer can use for licenses and sign-in. Sign in at
[key.plrs.im](https://key.plrs.im) with a code that Polaris Key emails you. That is where you see
your library, your devices and your account.

## Before you start

Use the email address you bought the app with. Licenses bought with that email can show up in
your library by themselves. A new email address creates your Polaris Key account the first time
you sign in. If you bought on Steam or in a store, see
[Bought with a different email or on Steam?](/docs/help/join-accounts/#bought-on-steam-or-in-a-store).

## Sign in with an email code

1. Go to [key.plrs.im](https://key.plrs.im).
2. Enter your email address in **Email**.
3. Select **Continue**.
4. Open the email from **Polaris Key**. Its subject starts with **Your Polaris Key code**.
5. On the **Check your email** page, type the 6-digit code. The page signs you in when you enter
   the last digit.

To use the link instead, select **Sign in** in the email. In the browser where you asked for the
code, the page that opens says **Sign in to Polaris Key** and **Continue as** your address. Select
**Sign in** on that page. Keep the **Check your email** page open: it signs in too. The code and
the link each work once, for 10 minutes.

## If you open the link on another device

A different browser shows a page that starts with **Confirm sign-in**, with a **Confirm** button.
Select **Confirm** only if you asked for the code. It confirms the sign-in in the browser where
you started, not in the one you are using. Go back to that browser. It signs in by itself.

## If you didn't ask for a code

A code you didn't ask for means someone typed your address. They can't sign in without the code, so
ignore the email and don't share the code. If a page asks you to **Confirm** a sign-in you didn't
start, don't select it. Close the page. If you also got a **Wasn't you?** email, or see changes you
didn't make, read [Secure your account](/docs/help/secure-your-account/).

## Other ways to sign in

If the page shows **Continue with single sign-on**, select it and sign in on the page that opens.

If the page shows **Have a license key?**, select it, enter the key, then sign in. The dialog to
activate a license opens with your key filled in. See [Activate your app](/docs/help/activate/).

A work or school email can take you to your organization's sign-in instead. See
[Sign in with your work account](/docs/help/work-account/).

## Check it worked

Your library opens. To sign out, open the account menu and select **Sign out**.

<HelpMessage id="step_up_required" />

Before some account changes, Polaris Key asks for a sign-in from the last 5 minutes. This
protects your account if someone else uses your device. It applies when you add or remove a
sign-in method, and when you delete your account.

In **Account**, **Confirm it's you first** offers these ways:

- A button that starts with **Use your passkey to**, when you have a passkey. To get a code instead,
  select **Use an email code instead**.
- A button that starts with **Email a code to**. Enter the 6-digit code, then select the button
  that starts with **Confirm and**.
- A button that starts with **Sign in again with**, when you have no passkey or email to use.

To delete your account, sign in again first. See [Delete your account](/docs/help/delete-account/).
