**Sync Across Devices** keeps your settings the same on every device, through your Polaris Locker account.

## What you need

You need to be signed in to the app with your Polaris Locker account. A device you added with only a license key keeps its settings on that device. They upload the first time you sign in.

## What syncs

Settings the app marks as yours, such as volume or layout, and, where the app supports it, other data it syncs. A setting your developer locked does not sync, and the lock wins on every device. Some apps keep a setting on one kind of device only, such as Windows.

## Your saves

If a save changed on two devices, the app shows **Two versions**. Choose **Keep this one**, **Keep the other** or **Keep both**. **Keep both** is unavailable when you have no free save slot; delete a save you don't need first.

Saves belong to the account that is signed in. Another person on the same device sees their own. If a save fails to send or load, choose **Try again**.

## Encrypted sync

Synced data is usually encrypted on your devices, so Polaris Locker can't read it. A new device needs your key before it syncs, and until then the app says **Sync is locked**. See [Unlock sync on a new device](/docs/help/sync-new-device/) and [Your recovery code](/docs/help/recovery-code/).

## If a change doesn't arrive

- Your devices pick up changes when the app syncs. Open the app on each device and wait a moment.
- If two devices change the same setting, the later change wins.
- If the developer has paused syncing, your changes stay on the device and send once it resumes.
- If you are over your storage, new changes that add data are refused. Clearing or deleting data always works. Nothing is deleted for you.

## If you removed the app's data

If you removed an app's data in Polaris Locker, your other devices show **You're signed out** at their next sync. Choose **Sign in to sync**: they start fresh, and changes you hadn't synced yet are gone.

## Your synced data in Polaris Locker

Open the app at [polaris.locker](https://polaris.locker): **Sync Across Devices** shows your storage and each device's last sync. To download or remove it, see [Synced data](/docs/help/manage-your-data/#synced-data).
