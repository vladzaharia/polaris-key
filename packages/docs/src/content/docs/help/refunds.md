This page helps with refunds, charges and cancellations for an app that uses Polaris Key, a service an app's developer can use for licenses, sign-in and downloads.

:::caution[Important]
Polaris Key takes no payment, so it can't refund an app. The app's developer or the store you bought it from can.
:::

## What happens to the app after a refund

- If a store refunds a purchase that unlocked something in an app, it stops working the next time the app checks your license. The App Store and Google Play tell Polaris Key about a refund. Polaris Key checks Steam about once a week.
- If the developer ends your license after a refund, your library shows **Suspended** for that license, and the app may say **License disabled**.
- A purchase the store has refunded can't be brought back with **Restore purchase** unless the store reverses the refund. See [Restore a purchase](/docs/help/restore-purchase/).

## Billing and cancellations

Polaris Key doesn't charge you, so it has nothing for you to cancel. To change or stop a charge, use the store where you pay, or ask the developer. Removing an app from your library doesn't cancel or refund it.
