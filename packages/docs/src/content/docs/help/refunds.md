:::caution[Important]
Polaris Locker takes no payment, so it can't refund an app. The app's developer or the store you bought it from can.
:::

## What happens to the app after a refund

- If a store refunds a purchase that unlocked something in an app, it stops working the next time the app checks your license. The App Store and Google Play tell Locker about a refund. Locker checks Steam about once a week.
- If the developer ends your license after a refund, your library shows **Suspended** for that license, and the app can say **License disabled** when you activate it, and **Signed out** while it runs. See [What your license status means](/docs/help/license-status/#suspended).
- A purchase the store has refunded can't be brought back with **Restore purchase** unless the store reverses the refund. See [Restore a purchase](/docs/help/restore-purchase/).

## Billing and cancellations

There is nothing to cancel in Locker. To change or stop a charge, or to ask for a refund, ask the store you paid, or the developer if you paid them directly. Not sure which? See [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/). Removing an app from your library doesn't cancel or refund it.
