Your library cannot show a full license key again, so look for the key where you got it. You may
not need it at all.

<Callout type="caution" title="Important">
  Polaris Key does not keep your full key, so your library cannot show it again.
  On the app's page, **License key** shows only the start of the key. Keep the
  copy from your email or the store.
</Callout>

## Check whether you still need the key

If the app is already in your library, the license stays there even if you lose the key. Open the
app's page in your library to see the license and manage its devices.

On a new device, use **Sign in** if the app offers it. Sign in with the account that holds the
license. You do not need the key.

## Find the key

1. Search your email for the message you got when you bought the app. The key is in it.
2. If you bought from a store or from the developer's website, look in the receipt or order details
   there.
3. Copy the whole key. It starts with `pkey_`.

## Get a replacement key

If you cannot find the key, ask the app's developer for a new one. Never send your full license key
to anyone.

## Check it worked

Activate the app with the key, or sign in. The app shows **Activated**. See
[Activate your app](/docs/help/activate/). To keep the license in your library, see
[Add a license with a key](/docs/help/add-a-license/).
