This page says in plain words what an app that uses Polaris Key reads from your device, what its
developer sees, and what you can ask for.

## What an app reads from your device

An app uses these details to count your devices against your license, notice a changed computer
and fix problems:

- **Hardware details**, such as your computer's model, processor, memory, network card and disk.
  The app scrambles each one on your device. Polaris Key never receives the original values. Apps
  that run in a web browser don't read hardware details.
- **System details**: your operating system and its version, your language and time zone, and the
  version of the app.
- **A few named apps**: a developer can ask whether specific apps, such as a companion app, are
  installed. The app can't list the apps you have.

An app doesn't read your user name, your files or your browsing. A developer can turn the
hardware check off.

## What the developer sees

- A random ID that exists for that app only. Another app sees a different ID for you.
- Your licenses for the app, its devices, and the kind of sign-in you used, such as Steam.
- Your email, only if the developer already has it from your purchase or you agree to share it.

A developer never sees your Polaris Key account ID, your other sign-in methods, your birth date or
anything from another app.

## What Polaris Key holds

Polaris Key holds the details you give your account: email addresses, sign-in methods, name,
picture and an optional birth date. You can change them in
[Your account and sign-in methods](/docs/help/account/).

## Ask for a copy or for erasure

- To ask what Polaris Key holds about your account, read
  [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/).
- To ask what a developer holds, such as your licenses and devices, ask the app's developer. Ask
  the developer too if you want it to erase its records.
- To erase your Polaris Key account, see [Delete your account](/docs/help/delete-account/).
  Deleting it doesn't erase a developer's own records.
