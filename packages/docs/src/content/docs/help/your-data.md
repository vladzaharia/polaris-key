Your library at key.plrs.im lets you download or remove information held for you.

## What is stored where

Your Polaris Key account holds your sign-in methods, name and picture, licenses, devices, where
you are signed in, and the apps in your library. Each app can also keep its own information about
you, such as saves or settings. The app's developer manages that information.

## Download my data

You can download everything from your account, or information from one app.

1. Open **Account**, then **Your data and privacy**, to download your full account file.
2. Open an app in your library to download a file for that app only.
3. Select **Download my data**.
4. Sign in again when asked. This protects your information if you left the site signed in.

Your full account file includes your account, sign-in methods, name and pictures, passkeys, where
you're signed in, apps connected to your account, accepted terms, library, licenses, each app's
information, and activity. An app's file includes only information held for you by that app.

## Remove my data from an app

Removing information from an app deletes the information Polaris Key holds for you in that app.

1. Open the app in your library.
2. Select **Remove my data**.
3. Sign in again when asked.
4. Choose whether to keep your license, then confirm.

Keeping your license keeps the app in your library. Its developer can still connect you to old
records through that license. Removing the license takes it out of your library too.

## Get a support code

A support code is a short code for one app. Read it to the app's support team so they can find the
right person without seeing your Polaris Key account.

1. Open the app in your library.
2. Select **Get a support code**.
3. Read the code to the app's support team.

Removing your information from that app ends the old code. Ask for another code if you need one.

## Delete your account

Deleting your account schedules it for deletion after 14 days. Until then, you stay signed in and
your licenses and devices keep working.

1. Open **Account**, then **Your data and privacy**.
2. Select **Delete account**.
3. Type your email address and sign in again when asked.
4. Confirm the deletion.

Choose **Keep my account** during the 14 days to cancel the deletion. After 14 days, Polaris Key
erases the account for good. Your licenses stay with each app's developer, and each developer
erases its own records.

Time Travel keeps deleted rows for up to 30 days. If a restore happens, Polaris Key applies the
deletion list again.
