Your library at key.plrs.im lets you see and download or remove information held for you. This
page says in plain words what an app that uses Polaris Key reads from your device, what its
developer sees, and what you can ask for.

## What an app reads from your device

- **Computer identifiers**, such as your computer's ID and serial number, its network card address
  and its disk ID. The app scrambles each one on your device. Polaris Key never receives the
  original values.
  - An app uses them to count your devices against your license, to give a free license to one
    computer only once, and to notice a changed computer.
  - Apps that run in a web browser don't read them.
- **System details**: your operating system and its version, your computer's model, processor,
  number of processor cores and memory, your language and time zone, and the version of the app.
  Polaris Key receives these as they are, and the app's developer can see them.
  - They let the developer see which systems the app runs on, check that it works on yours and send
    it the right settings.
  - Apps that run in a web browser send the kind of operating system, processor cores, memory,
    language, time zone and the version of the app.
- **Your computer's name**, as set in your system settings. It can include your own name, and you
  can change it there. Apps you install usually send it, so you can tell your devices apart in your
  library. The developer sees it too. Apps that run in a web browser send none.
- **A few named apps**: a developer can ask whether specific apps, such as a companion app, are
  installed. The app can't list the apps you have.

An app doesn't read your user name on the computer, your files or your browsing. A developer can
turn the computer identifiers off. The system details are still sent.

## What the developer sees

- A random ID that exists for that app only. Another app sees a different ID for you.
- Your licenses for the app, its devices with their system details and names, and the kind of
  sign-in you used, such as Steam.
- Your screen name and picture, only if you agree to share them.
- Your email, only if the developer already has it from your purchase or you agree to share it.

A developer never sees your Polaris Key account ID, your other sign-in methods, your birth date or
anything from another app.

## What Polaris Key holds

Polaris Key holds the details you give your account: email addresses, sign-in methods, name,
picture and an optional birth date. It also keeps your licenses, the apps in your library, a list
of the browsers signed in to your account and a record of changes to your sign-in methods.
For each device, it keeps the scrambled computer identifiers, never the originals, until the device
is removed. It keeps your computer's name and the last system details your device sent for as long
as the license exists. Each app's developer manages its own information about you, such as saves
or settings. You can change your account details in
[Your account and sign-in methods](/docs/help/account/).

## Download my data

You can download everything from your account, or information from one app.

1. Open **Account**, then **Your data**, to download your full account file.
2. Open an app in your library to download a file for that app only.
3. Select **Download my data**.
4. Sign in again when asked. This protects your information if you left the site signed in.

Your full account file includes your account, sign-in methods, name and pictures, passkeys, where
you've signed in, connected apps, accepted terms, library, licenses, package access details, joined
accounts, each app's information, and activity. If the activity list is long, the file keeps recent
entries and says it was shortened. An app's file includes only information it holds for you.

## Remove my data from an app

Removing information from an app deletes the information Polaris Key holds for you in that app.

1. Open the app in your library.
2. Select **Remove my data**.
3. Sign in again when asked.
4. Choose whether to keep your license, then confirm.

Keeping your license keeps the app in your library. Its developer can still connect you to old
records through that license. Removing the license takes it out of your library too.

## Get a support code

A support code is a short code for one app. Read it to the app's support team so they can find the
right person without seeing your Polaris Key account.

1. Open the app in your library.
2. Select **Get a support code**.
3. Read the code to the app's support team.

Removing your information from that app ends the old code. Ask for another code if you need one.

## Delete your account

Deleting your account schedules it for erasure 14 days later. Until then, you stay signed in and
your licenses and devices keep working. For the full steps, see
[Delete your account](/docs/help/delete-account/).

1. Open **Account**, then **Your data**.
2. Select **Delete account**.
3. Type your email address, then select **Schedule deletion**. Sign in again when asked.

During the 14 days, select **Cancel scheduled deletion** in **Your data** to keep your account.
After 14 days, Polaris Key erases the account for good. Your licenses stay with each app's
developer, and each developer erases its own records.

While a deletion is scheduled, you cannot join another account to this one. Cancel the deletion
first, then join them.

Polaris Key keeps database backups for up to 30 days, used only after a serious fault. If a backup
brings back an account deleted after it was taken, no one can sign in to it, and a separate record
with only an internal reference and date makes the next scheduled cleanup erase it again. This
usually takes about 15 minutes, or longer if many accounts need cleanup.

A deletion still within its 14-day waiting period is not in that record. If a restore predates your
request, schedule deletion again.

## Ask about or erase your data

- Polaris Key doesn't publish a privacy address. [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/) says
  who to ask about your Polaris Key account.
- To ask what an app holds about you, such as your licenses and devices, ask the app's developer.
  Ask the developer too if you want it to erase its records.
- To erase your Polaris Key account, see [Delete your account](/docs/help/delete-account/).
  Deleting it doesn't erase a developer's own records.
