**Pair with Someone Nearby** connects your device to a device of someone you are with, once. After that, the app can share things between the two of you, such as a file or a save, without pairing again. Your accounts stay separate. Nothing moves between them except what you choose to send.

## Pair

You and the other person both need the same app, on the same Wi-Fi network, in the same room.

1. In the app, open **Pair with Someone Nearby**. The other person does the same.
2. Your screens each show a **six-digit code**. Check that the code is the same on both.
3. Check the list of what the app asks to share.
4. If the codes match and you agree, select **Confirm**. If not, select **Decline**.

When you both confirm, the screen says **Paired**. If only one of you confirms, nothing is saved.

## What you share

Pairing shares nothing by itself. It lets the two devices find each other. The app shows the kinds of data it wants to share, such as saves or photos, before you confirm, and only what you both agree to can be sent. Each time one of you sends something, the other person is asked first.

The name the other device shows is only what that device reports, so rely on the code and on being in the same room.

## Remove a pairing

1. Open the app and find **Pair with Someone Nearby**.
2. Select the person's device, then **Remove**.

The pairing ends on your device, and anything still being set up or only partly sent stops. The other person's app shows the device as not reachable. To pair again, repeat the steps above.

## Pair with someone you are already paired with

You can pair again. The new pairing takes over once the two devices connect for the first time. Until then, the old one keeps working. If you did not start it, remove it.

## Pairing is off

If the app says **Pairing is off**, the app's developer has not turned it on, or it is not available yet. Nothing is wrong with your device. Try again later, or [get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/).

## If the other device is not found

- Check that you are on the same Wi-Fi network.
- Allow the app to find devices on your local network. See [Nearby Devices](/docs/help/nearby-devices/).
- Open **Pair with Someone Nearby** on both devices within five minutes of each other. The window closes by itself after five minutes.
