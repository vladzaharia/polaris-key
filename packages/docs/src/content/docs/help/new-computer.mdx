To use an app on a new computer, activate it there. If your license is already on every device it
allows, free the old computer first.

## Before you start

Have your license key, or the account you sign in with. If you lost the key, see
[Find a lost license key](/docs/help/lost-key/).

## What happens when you free a computer

The old computer stops counting toward your limit, and its app asks to be activated again. Its saved
files stay on it. You can add it back later. See [Manage your devices](/docs/help/devices/).

## If you still have the old computer

1. On the old computer, open the screen in the app that shows your account and license.
2. Select **Sign out**. Do this while the computer is online, so the app can tell Polaris Key.
3. On the new computer, open the app and activate it. See [Activate your app](/docs/help/activate/).

Your app may look different. If you cannot find **Sign out**, or the old computer was offline, free
it from your library as described next.

## If the old computer is gone

1. Sign in at [key.plrs.im](https://key.plrs.im) and open the app's page.
2. In **Devices**, select **Remove** next to the old computer, then select **Remove** again.
3. On the new computer, open the app and activate it.

If the license is not in your library yet, add its key first. See
[Add a license with a key](/docs/help/add-a-license/). If the app says the device limit was reached,
see [Device limit reached](/docs/help/device-limit/).

## If you reinstalled the same computer

Activate the app again with your key or by signing in. If the app checks the device, the new install
replaces the old one. If **Devices** lists the same computer twice, remove the older one.

<HelpMessage id="hardware_mismatch" />

Activate the app again on this computer. This does not use a second device, because the old entry
for this computer is replaced.

## Check it worked

Your new computer appears under **Devices** in your library, and the app shows **Activated**.

## Undo

To go back, free the new computer the same way, then activate the old one again.
