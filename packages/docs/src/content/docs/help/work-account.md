Some organizations, such as an employer or a school, sign their people in to Polaris Locker, the
service the app uses for licenses and sign-in. When your email belongs to one of them, typing it
takes you to your organization's sign-in instead of an emailed code.

:::caution[Important]
Locker and the app's developer can't change your organization's sign-in or reset its
password. Your organization's IT help desk can.
:::

## Sign in

1. On the sign-in page, enter your work or school email and select **Continue**.
2. The page shows your organization's name and your email. Select **Continue to
   _Organization_**, where _Organization_ is its name.
3. Sign in on your organization's page, the same way you do at work or school.
4. You come back signed in. The first time, you also set up your account.

If your Locker account already uses that email, your organization's sign-in is added to it,
and you get an email saying so.

To use another email, select **Change** next to your email.

## Get a code instead

Some organizations let you choose. The page then says, for example, "students.example.edu signs
in through Lakeside University." and also offers **Email me a code instead**. Select it, and we
email you a 6-digit code, the same as for any other email.

## When your organization requires its own sign-in

The page says, for example, "Lakeside University requires its own sign-in for example.edu
addresses." There is no code option: codes we email to that address don't sign you in. A passkey
on your account still works, and so do the other ways you added to sign in.

## When your organization's sign-in doesn't work

1. Select **Continue to _Organization_** again.
2. If your organization's page shows an error or doesn't load, wait a few minutes and try again.
3. If it keeps failing, contact your organization's IT help desk.

## Check it worked

You're back where you started, signed in.
