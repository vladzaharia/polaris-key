A license can show up in your library when the email on it is verified on your Polaris Key
account. If you bought with a different email, add that email to your account.

## Add the email you bought with

1. Open the account menu and select **Sign-in methods**.
2. Under **Email**, select **Add an email**.
3. In **Email address**, enter the address.
4. Select **Send code**.
5. If you see **Confirm it's you first**, confirm it's you. Polaris Key then sends the code.
6. Enter the 6-digit code from the email. Polaris Key adds the address when you enter the last
   digit.

## Check it worked

The address appears under **Email**. Licenses bought with it can join your library by
themselves. Polaris Key emails every verified address on your account.

## If you used Hide My Email

If you signed in with Apple and chose **Hide My Email**, Polaris Key has a private address that
can't match your real email. The page shows **Bought something with your real email?** Select
**Add your real email**, then follow the steps above.

## If another account uses the address

You find out after you enter the code. Polaris Key never merges two accounts because they share
an email address.

When a sign-in method belongs to another account, the app can show this message:

<HelpMessage id="link_conflict" />

In your account the page says **Another Polaris Key account uses this email. Link that account
instead, or use a different email.** For Apple, Google or Steam it says the account is connected
to another Polaris Key account. Sign in to the account that has it, or connect a different one.

When another account already uses the email, the app can show this message:

<HelpMessage id="email_in_use" />

When you sign in, the page says **A Polaris Key account already uses this email address, but not
as a way to sign in.** Sign in with the method you used before. Then add the email under
**Sign-in methods**.

## If an account is scheduled for deletion

You can't join two accounts while either one is scheduled for deletion. Open **Your data** in
**Account** on that account and select **Cancel scheduled deletion**, then join them again. See
[Delete your account](/docs/help/delete-account/).

## Bought on Steam or in a store?

Connecting Steam under **Sign-in methods** adds it as a sign-in method on your account. For a store
purchase, open the app. If it offers **Restore purchase**, select it. See
[Activate your app](/docs/help/activate/).

## Still need help?

If you don't know which account has the address, if your licenses are in two accounts, or if a
license is still missing, read [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/).
