Some apps sign you in by themselves. When you open the app, it uses the account you already use on your device, such as your Google or Apple account, and your Polaris Locker account and license come with it. You don't type a code.

## What it needs

You have to link that account to Polaris Locker once. After you sign in to an app the usual way, the app can offer to **Link** the account. Select **No thanks** and it doesn't ask again for that account.

The first time an app signs you in this way, it shows what it uses, such as your name and license, and asks you to continue. Nothing is shared until you select **Continue**.

Linking emails you, so you know when an account was linked.

## Apple works per developer

Apple gives every developer a different identifier for you. A Sign in with Apple link made in one developer's app works only in that developer's apps. In another developer's app, Apple shows a different account, and you link it there too.

## If the account is already linked elsewhere

If it says **This Google account is linked to another Polaris Locker account**, the account belongs to a different Locker account. Nothing changed. See [Bought with a different email or on Steam?](/docs/help/join-accounts/#bought-on-steam-or-in-a-store).

## Stop it

- **On the device.** Select **Sign out** in the app. It stays signed out, and doesn't sign you in again by itself, until you sign in yourself.
- **For the account.** Open the account menu, select **Sign-in methods**, and remove the Google or Apple account. See [Sign-in methods](/docs/help/account/#sign-in-methods).

## Still need help?

If the app asks you to sign in every time, read [Sign in to Polaris Locker](/docs/help/sign-in/). For anything else, read [Get help with an app](/docs/help/contact/).
