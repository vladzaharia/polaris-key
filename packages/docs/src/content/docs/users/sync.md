For products that turn it on, Cloud Sync keeps your settings and saves the same on every device
you use. Not every product offers it; if yours doesn't, settings and saves stay on each device.

## What syncs

- **Settings** you choose: a volume, key bindings, a theme. Settings the game keeps for you sync
  too.
- **Saves**: up to 16 slots per game, each with its thumbnail, playtime and progress.

Some settings stay on one device by design, such as a window size. A setting the product locks
shows its fixed value everywhere.

## It needs sign-in

Cloud Sync works only while you're signed in through the product. A device you activated with a
license key alone keeps its settings on that device; they upload the first time you sign in, and a
newer change from your other devices wins.

If you sign out with changes that haven't uploaded, they stay on the device for 30 days and upload
when you sign back in to the same account.

## What the messages mean

| You see                  | What it means                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Waiting to sync**      | Your change is kept on this device and uploads as soon as it can: you're offline, syncing is paused, or your storage is full. |
| **Saved on this device** | Your save is safe here and uploads later, for the same reasons.                                                               |

You don't need to do anything: the product keeps trying.

## When your storage is full

How much you can keep depends on your license. When it's full, **nothing is deleted**: new
changes stay on this device and sync once there is room, for example after a license upgrade. You
can still read, clear and delete what's already synced.

## When two devices disagree

For most settings, the latest change wins. When two devices change one save slot, the game shows
both and you choose which to keep.

## Your synced data

Your settings and saves stay in your Cloud Sync data until you delete them or delete your account.
Removing a product from your library, or disconnecting a device, doesn't delete them. See
[The customer portal](/docs/users/portal/#deleting-your-account).
