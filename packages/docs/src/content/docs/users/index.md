:::note[Who this section is for]
Everything under **For users** is written in the second person, addressed directly to someone
*using* a product built on Polaris Key — not to the person operating it. If you support
customers, read these pages as what your customers see: they explain activation, the customer
portal, devices, updates, and privacy from that seat, in plain language. For how the platform
works underneath, see [Start here](/docs/start/) and [Services](/docs/services/core/).
:::

Depending on the product, using it might mean nothing more than pasting in a key once and never
thinking about it again — or it might mean signing in on the web to manage several licenses
across several products. This section covers every surface you might run into, from either end.

## In this section

| [Updates](/docs/users/updates/) | How new versions reach you, and why an old build might ask you to update. |

| [Privacy](/docs/users/privacy/) | What the product collects from your device, and why. |

Not every product turns on every surface described here — a license key might be all one product
ever asks for, while another adds sign-in, a portal, and auto-updates on top. If a page describes
something your product doesn't have, it simply doesn't apply to you.

## Getting help

Polaris Key is the platform underneath a product, not the product itself — for anything beyond
[Troubleshooting](/docs/users/troubleshooting/), the people who support the product you're using
are the right first stop.
