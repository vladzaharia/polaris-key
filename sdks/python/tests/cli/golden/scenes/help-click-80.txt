Usage: tidewater [OPTIONS] COMMAND [ARGS]...

Options:
  --help  Show this message and exit.

Commands:
  export  Export a mix

License:
  activate [key]                  Add a license key (prompted, or piped on
                                  stdin)
  status                          Show your license, devices and offline time
  deactivate                      Stop using your license on this device and
                                  make room for another
  login                           Sign in with your account in the browser
  logout                          Sign out on this device
  devices list                    List the devices using your license
  devices rename <deviceId> [label...]
                                  Rename a device (no name clears it)
  devices deauthorize <deviceId>  Remove a device and make room for another
  update check                    Check for a newer version
  update apply                    Download and install the newer version
