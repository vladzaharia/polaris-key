### The delete cascade, in its exact order (after U-12.1 and U-12.2)

`DELETE /api/products/{product}/data`:

0. **Gates.** Session → CSRF → product linked (else 404) → sign-in ≤ 5 min old → licences removable (else 409) → 5 per minute.
1. **Licences out**, only with `alsoRemoveLicense`: `detachLicense` for each.
2. **Subject lookup.** No subject: done.
3. **Write-ahead.** Record `subject-erasures/<product>/<subject>` in BLOBS, create-only (`{v:1, product, subject, erasedAt}`). It must not live under `erasures/`, because the account replay reads every key there as an account id. A failed write answers 503 and nothing has been removed.
4. **Fence.** `clearDeviceSubjects({kind:"subject"}, "product_removed")`: the bindings for the subject and its aliases go, devices get `account_required`, and the ring lifecycle drops their wraps.
5. **Stores**, fault-isolated, in registry name order:
   1. `config.accountOverrides`
   2. `connect.rings`
   3. `core.entitlementEvents`
   4. `core.keyring`: wraps, adopted roots, rings. This removes the last E2E key material.
   5. `core.provisionedSecretMoves`
   6. `identity.restore`
   7. `sync.cloudSync`. Inside `blockConcurrencyWhile`, the DO:
      - deletes the SSE-C keys;
      - deletes every R2 key it lists, 1,000 at a time;
      - sweeps `u/<p>/<s>/` and the prefixes of subjects merged into it;
      - calls `deleteAlarm`, then `storage.deleteAll`;
      - writes the `erased_at` marker, which refuses pushes for 24 h, and its cleanup alarm.

      Then the `sync_directory` row goes.

6. **Commit**, one batch: `subject.deleted`, support codes, aliases, the subject row.
7. **Audit** `account.product.remove`.

**Idempotency and resume.**

- Every step deletes by key or is create-only.
- If step 5 fails, the answer is 200 `{removed: true, pending: true}` and the subject row stays.
- "A ledger entry plus a live subject row" means a removal is owed. The pass (15-minute tick and nightly, 25 per pass, cursor in KV) re-runs steps 4–7. So does the person's own retry. Step 1 is never replayed.
- **After a D1 restore:** the restored subject row matches its ledger entry, and the same re-run removes the restored overrides, ring and directory rows. D1 Time Travel does not touch DO storage, so the DO stays erased.
- **After a DO point-in-time restore:** the operator runs the full pass for that product.
- Ledger entries are swept after 35 days, which is past the 30-day windows of both D1 Time Travel and DO point-in-time recovery.

**Account erasure** keeps its state machine (ledger, close, store rounds, licences, avatars, commit, back-off sweeper, replay). The only change is that the device fence moves before the store rounds.

**Console deletes:**

- I-12's per-subject delete becomes isolated.
- U-11a's Cloud Sync delete runs ring, then DO.

### What the export contains, and its size limits

**JSON:** `GET /api/products/{product}/export`, schema `pkey-product-export/1`. The product sections of `/api/me/export` get the same bounds. `stores["sync.cloudSync"]` carries:

- the directory counters, usage, `encryption` and `encryptionSince`;
- the settings rows (at most 256 and 64 KiB by the store's own caps; E2E rows as ciphertext plus `kid`);
- per-collection counts and bytes;
- records inline only while they total ≤ 4 MiB; otherwise `recordsIn: "zip"`;
- file metadata up to 2,000 rows; otherwise `filesIn: "zip"`;
- chunk count and bytes;
- clients as `clientId`, `deviceId` and `lastSeen`.

`core.keyring` and `config.accountOverrides` are unchanged: no sealed values, and secrets are named only.

**Zip:** `?format=zip` returns `polaris-locker-<product>-data.zip` containing:

- `README.txt` and `data.json` (the JSON document above);
- `stores/sync.cloudSync/records/<collection>.ndjson`: every live row, tombstone and parked copy, fetched 500 rows per DO page;
- `files.json` plus `files/<n>.bin`: live files and kept revisions. E2E files are the stored ciphertext; server-mode files are plaintext read through SSE-C.
- `chunks/<collection>.bin` plus `chunks.json`: one container per collection, with an offset index.

Entry names are indexes, never blinded ids.

**Zip limits:**

- STORED entries with data descriptors, zip32.
- At most 4 GiB in total. The per-person ceiling of about 1.06 GiB keeps every export far below that.
- At most 65,000 entries; beyond that the answer is `413 export_too_large`, never a silent cut.
- Memory stays at one page or one R2 read at a time.
- CRC-32 costs about 1–3 s of CPU per GiB, inside the 30 s default.
- The gates match the JSON route: CSRF, step-up, 10 per minute, audit.

## 4. Decisions (★ = recommended)

- **D1 — The delete action in the card.**
  - ★ The card's action is "Remove my data from <Product>", reusing I-11's whole-product route.
  - Alternative: a Cloud-Sync-only `DELETE /api/products/{p}/sync`, as mockup 31 draws it. The next push from any signed-in device undoes it, so it cannot keep the promise "deleted".
- **D2 — Cascade order.**
  - ★ Fence first, keys before bytes, in both the per-product and account paths, with the console aligned to ring first.
  - Alternative: today's order (stores, then bindings) and CC-01's (DO, then ring).
- **D3 — When a store fails during removal.**
  - ★ Answer 200 `pending: true` and let the pass finish the removal.
  - Alternative: answer 503 and rely on the person to retry.
- **D4 — Restore coverage.**
  - ★ The cron pass covers D1 restores; a DO restore needs the operator's full pass. The residual (up to one tick after a restore) is recorded in THREAT.
  - Alternative: a per-request subject fence (an R2 HEAD at sign-in and when resolving the sync principal).
- **D5 — Ledger retention.**
  - ★ 35 days.
  - Alternative: forever, like the account ledger. Subjects are random, so they cannot be re-minted and need no permanent tombstone.
- **D6 — Export format.**
  - ★ `?format=zip` on the existing path, with stores appending entries through `exportEntries`.
  - Alternative A: a separate `/api/products/{p}/sync/export` behind a hook.
  - Alternative B: stay JSON-only and leave file bytes to the SDK export.
- **D7 — Which file bytes go in the zip.**
  - ★ Live files and kept revisions.
  - Alternative: live files only.
- **D8 — Export schema.**
  - ★ Reshape `stores["sync.cloudSync"]` inside `/1`: no product has Sync on, and the schema gets documented.
  - Alternative: bump to `pkey-product-export/2`.
- **D9 — Device sync time.**
  - ★ Stamp the pull hourly and show the time only; no "Up to date".
  - Alternative: push only, labelled "Last change sent".
- **D10 — ⌘K action.**
  - ★ Offer it only when the top match's product view is cached with `services.sync`.
  - Alternative: add `cloudSync: boolean` to `PortalLibrary` items.
- **D11 — `subject.deleted.accountOverridesRemoved`.**
  - ★ Defer it; it changes the developer feed.
  - Alternative: add it in U-12.1.
