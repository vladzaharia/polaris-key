### A. The rule (binding)

It applies to **every sign-in that binds a device**: device code and its QR
(`verification_uri_complete`), the web redirect (`authorize`), the native redirect (I-15, same
card), sign-in on a device that already runs on a licence (I-09 Q1), and the legacy product-OIDC
path (I-26, until I-08 replaces it). After the person authenticates, and before "Continue to
<App>", Core works out the person's choices.

1. **Candidates.** These are the account's licences for the product that pass `licenseUsable`,
   plus the licences where I-24a makes the person a seat holder. Licences with `superseded_by` set
   are excluded once LX-08 adds the column. Each candidate carries `seats {used, limit}`:
   - `used` counts seat-holding devices (`authorized`, `last_seen ≥ seatActiveSince(now)`);
   - `limit` is `licenseDeviceLimit`.
2. **Row states.**
   - **Free:** `used < limit` and a dry-run `authorizeDevice` passes. The row is enabled.
   - **Full:** the row has no radio and reads "No free devices". It offers **Replace a device**
     (§B) and the **Free a device** link to PX-10's focused flow
     (`/#/p/<slug>/free-device?license=<licenseId>&for=<this device label>&return=/signin?request=<handle>`;
     `for=` is PX-10's device label, §F.5).
   - **Blocked:** the licence would refuse for another reason, for example fingerprint `strict` on
     a device-code mint. The row is disabled with a one-line reason.
   - Expired, refunded and disabled licences are not listed.
   - **Sign-in** licences (`access: "account"`, §F.6) keep their device limit (owner,
     2026-10-05): free or full by the same seat count as any licence.
3. **The step is shown whenever there is at least one candidate**, free or full. That includes a
   single free licence, which is a one-tap confirm.
   - **Preselection.** If the device already runs on one of the candidates, that row is
     preselected and tagged "On this device" (`current: true`). If it runs on a usable licence
     that is not a candidate (key-entered or floating), the row **Keep the license this device
     uses** is preselected (I-09 Q1). Otherwise the first free candidate in `anchorPolicy` order
     is preselected: I-09's inline order, then LX-10's `chooseAnchor` order.
   - **No exception for rank.** A higher-rank free candidate is listed first but never
     preselected over the device's current licence or Keep (§F.2). Preselection never moves a
     device, and never binds anything.
4. **No candidates at all.**
   - When the product's policy grants this person a tier (`identityTier` / `autoIssue`), the step
     shows one preselected **New** row (the product name, the tier pill, "Created when you continue")
     and the primary **Use this license and continue**. The new licence is minted on that press
     and attached to the account (origin `oidc`; I-09 §2.4 step 3).
   - Otherwise the card reads "No <Product> license in this account" (SIGN-IN.md §3.6 rule 6).
     When the product accepts keys (`getLicense.keyEntry`) it takes the key inline, with PX-17's
     confirm and the primary **Add and use on this device**; it offers **Get <Product>** when
     `getLicense.purchaseUrl` is set, then **Use another account** and **Cancel**.
5. **No second auto-issued licence while a usable one exists.** The only exception is the explicit
   row **Create a new free license**, offered only when (a) the policy would auto-issue to this
   person and (b) every candidate is full (delegated decision 1). It is never preselected.
6. **Binding happens only at Continue, on the person's explicit choice.** Core refuses a Continue
   that carries no choice whenever the step was shown.

### B. Replace a device (owner follow-up)

A full row is not a dead end. **Replace a device** expands the row in place and lists that
licence's **seat-holding** devices. Dormant devices hold no seat and are not listed. For each
device the row shows:

- its label, a platform glyph and "last used <when>";
- **Least recent** on the least recently used device, which is preselected;
- **Active now** on a device seen in the last 10 minutes;
- **This browser** on the browser's own web device, when there is one.

Picking a device shows one inline confirm that names both devices: "Replace Work laptop?",
"Work laptop signs out of <Product> and <this device> takes its seat. Work laptop can sign in again
later if a seat is free. We'll email you about it.", with **Replace and continue** and **Back**
(SIGN-IN.md §3.7). Nothing is written before the flow's final primary. Then one request frees that
device and binds the new installation to the freed seat, in one D1 batch (§F.3). There is no trip
to the portal.

**The same operation as the portal's Remove.** I-08 extracts `freeAccountDevice()` from
`handleDeviceDelete` (`services/identity/portal/api.ts`, "async function handleDeviceDelete").
`DELETE /api/licenses/<p>/<id>/devices/<deviceId>` and the card's Continue both call it, with
identical rules:

- the product's portal is on (`portal_enabled = 1`); otherwise the card offers no Replace;
- the account owns the licence (`getPortalLicense`). On a seat-holder licence (I-24a) the card
  shows only the Free a device link;
- the rate limit `portalDeviceDisconnect` (20 per 60 s, keyed `<product>:<account>:<ip>`) is
  charged after ownership is proven, and **both surfaces share one budget**;
- the device belongs to that licence;
- `setDeviceStatus(…, "deauthorized")` and `deleteTokenRecord`;
- the audit row `portal.device.disconnect`, whose summary gains "to sign in <new label>";
- the `deviceRemovedNotice` security email.

**No release cap or cooldown exists today.** The Worker has no per-licence release cap and no
cooldown on freeing devices. This was checked in the portal DELETE handler, `core/devices.ts` and
`repo.ts`; `SEAT_DORMANCY_SECONDS` is dormancy, not a cooldown. A cap added later goes into
`freeAccountDevice`, so both surfaces apply it.

- **Rate limit hit.** The card API answers `429 rate_limited` with `retryAfter` (the seconds left
  in the window), and the card reads "Too many device changes. Try again in <n> seconds."
- **Race.** The free and the seat claim commit in one batch, so a race normally leaves both
  undone: Continue answers `device_limit` with a fresh view and nothing changed. Only if
  `authorizeDevice`'s claim cannot join the batch does the freed device stay freed (audited and
  emailed); the card then reads "<device> was removed, but someone took the seat first. Choose
  again." (§F.3).

### C. Wire impact

**Device wire: none.** None of these change:

- `PROTOCOL_VERSION` (stays **4**), `DISCOVERY_VERSION` (2) and `corpusVersion` (2);
- any `typ`, claim, document member, discovery member, device route or poll status.

The device-code poll stays `pending` while the person chooses, and `ready` mints the token on the
chosen licence. P1-07's `confirm.attachable` is computed as today, except that it is `false` when
the person picked an account licence other than the device's current one: attach is offered only
after **Keep**. The redirect code record (§2.5) carries the choice, and `redirect/token` answers
for that licence.

**Card API.** These routes are browser-facing, same-origin and `no-store`. They need the account
session and PX-W13's `__Host-pk_req` binder, and they are documented in WIRE-CONTRACT-V4 §12.4 and
§12.7. They live in `@polaris-key/protocol/identity`. `shared-jws` and `client-core` do not change.

- **`GET /api/signin/requests/:handle/licenses`** answers `LicenseChoiceView`:
  - `state`: `"choose" | "autoIssue" | "none"`;
  - `choices: LicenseChoice[]`;
  - `keep: boolean` (true only when the device runs on a usable licence that is **not** in
    `choices`);
  - `preselected: string | "keep" | null`;
  - `create: {tierName, access} | null`;
  - `getLicense: {activateUrl, purchaseUrl | null, keyEntry: boolean} | null`.

  A `LicenseChoice` is `{id, tierName, name | null, origin, access: "seats" | "account",
seats: {used, limit | null}, current, expiresAt | null, state: "free" | "full" | "blocked",
blockedReason?, replace: {allowed, retryAfter | null} | null, freeDeviceUrl | null}`. `origin`
  is one of `purchase`, `store`, `key`, `free`, `developer` or `signin`. It is derived from
  `licenses.origin` and, after LX-08, `source`. `current` is true on the licence this device runs
  on. `access` is `"account"` for a licence issued by signing in (no key): the fact behind the
  origin "From signing in" and the mixed rule, never a displayed type. Its `seats` and `state`
  follow the same seat count as any licence (§F.6). Everything here is display data and never authorises anything.

- **`GET /api/signin/requests/:handle/licenses/:licenseId/devices`** answers `ReplaceView`:
  `{licenseId, seats, devices: [{id, label | null, platform | null, deviceType | null, lastSeen,
leastRecent, activeNow, thisBrowser}], replace: {allowed, retryAfter | null}}`. A licence the
  account does not own answers `404 not_found`; a sign-in licence answers like any other.
- **I-08's Continue `POST`** gains `choice: LicenseChoiceInput`, one of:
  - `{kind: "license", licenseId, replaceDeviceId?}`;
  - `{kind: "keep"}`;
  - `{kind: "create"}`.

  It answers `409 license_choice_required` when the choice is missing, or is `keep` or `create`
  without being offered. It answers `404 not_found` for a licence or device that is not the
  person's, `device_limit` for a race, and `429 rate_limited` with `retryAfter`. Bodies use the
  nested `PolarisErrorBody`.

- **PX-W13's consent route** takes `?choice=` and reports the chosen licence as `anchor`. It no
  longer runs a dry-run anchor.
- **`errors.json`** gains one code, `license_choice_required` (409, identity, registered by
  I-08). `gen:constants` carries it into every SDK, and no SDK branches on it. The other answers
  reuse `not_found`, `device_limit` and `rate_limited`.

**Transcripts.** I-08 records these in `scenarios/identity.ts`, and `gen:transcripts` writes the
Swift and Godot mirrors:

| File                               | Steps                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `devicecode-choose.json`           | The account holds two licences. The view preselects in rank order; the person picks the other licence; the poll reaches `ready` on the picked licence. |
| `devicecode-replace.json`          | The only licence is full. `ReplaceView` → Replace with the least recent device → `ready`. The replaced device's next refresh answers 401.              |
| `devicecode-choice-required.json`  | Continue without a choice → 409. The poll stays `pending`.                                                                                             |
| `devicecode-autoissue.json`        | No licence; the view's state is `autoIssue`; Continue → `ready` on a new licence attached to the account. Repeating it mints nothing new.              |
| `redirect-web-choose.json`         | The same choice through `authorize`, then `redirect/token`.                                                                                            |
| `devicecode-happy.json` (existing) | Re-recorded by I-08 as already planned. If its fixture account holds no licence, it now shows the `autoIssue` step.                                    |

LX-10's `signin-anchor.json` now asserts the **preselection** order and binding to the explicit
pick. **Corpus:** none (`pnpm gen:corpus -- --check` is unchanged). **Parity:** no new row.
`identity.devicecode` gains the note "choice on the card".

**F.6, Sign-in licences.**

> **Owner decision (2026-10-05): no 'Account-wide' label; origin shown as plain words (store
> named with the key).** Verbatim: "Maybe not even 'Account-wide' since all licenses are going to
> be inevitably account-bound." and "If the key came from Steam/storefront, we should include
> that." Every licence is account-bound, so none is labelled by type; the earlier "Account-wide"
> label is withdrawn.

> **Owner decision (2026-10-05): sign-in (OIDC) licenses stay device-limited.** Verbatim: "OIDC
> should still be device-limited, and an administrator can change the numbers as needed." This
> replaces the earlier proposal, in this section and decision 21, that `authorizeDevice` skip the
> seat count for a sign-in licence. The seat rule, the licence document and every limit stay as
> they are today (SIGN-IN.md D-53).

- **Definition.** A licence is a **sign-in licence** (`access: "account"`) when it is held by an account
  and has no licence key (no `license_keys` row, live or revoked): today's "Signed-in app"
  licences, `sub` set and no key, the rule the portal already uses (`model/library.ts`
  `licenseStatus`). Its devices are the devices signed in to the holding account
  (`devices.subject` → that account, `bound_by = 'signin'`). Every other licence is a seat licence
  (`access: "seats"`). The classification is derived; no tier column marks it (the proposed LX-08
  `tiers.device_access` is dropped).
- **Enforcement: unchanged.** `authorizeDevice` counts a sign-in licence's devices against
  its effective device limit exactly as for a seat licence. It can be full; the card then shows it
  as a Full row with **Replace a device** and **Free a device**, and `ReplaceView` answers for it.
  **Remove** frees its seat and signs that device out of the product.
- **Who sets the number.** The operator, with the controls that exist today: the product's default
  device limit and the tier's device limit; LX-14a adds a per-licence limit that beats the tier
  (SIGN-IN.md D-53 names the console and admin API surfaces).
- **Wire.** Card API: `access` beside the real `seats: {used, limit}`; `create.access`. Portal API
  (`GET /api/licenses`, `GET /api/library`): each licence summary gains `access`; the library's
  `seats` keeps the real limit. Both are browser-facing, same-origin, additive and documented in
  WIRE-CONTRACT-V4 §12.7 and OpenAPI (rule 10, `routeCoverage`). **Device wire: none.** The
  licence document keeps its shape and its `deviceLimit` entitlement. `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 4,
  `corpusVersion` 2, no corpus file.
- **Display** (SIGN-IN.md §3.6, PORTAL.md §5.3): the tier is a neutral pill with "2 of 5 devices"
  beside it for every licence, and the seat meter; the meta line gives the origin in plain words
  and the term: "From signing in", "Key ending 3WPLDA" (else "Added with a key"), "Steam key
  ending 3WPLDA" when the key came from a store purchase (store name only, from License's
  `licenseProvenance`), "From Steam" for a store-bound licence with no key, "Gift", "Included
  with <org>", then "Lifetime", "Renews 3 Mar" or "Expires 24 Dec". No licence is labelled by
  type. When an account holds both a seat licence and a sign-in licence for one product, the seat
  licences hide their device counter and meter (a full one still says "No free devices";
  SIGN-IN.md D-54). The device list with **Remove** shows for both.
- **Owners.** I-09: the classification helper `licenseAccess(db, license)` in `core/anchor.ts`
  (display only; no change to `authorizeDevice`). I-08: `access` on the card API. The portal
  summaries and the portal UI change on a portal fix branch (SIGN-IN.md §10).
- **Transcripts.** I-08 adds `devicecode-sign-in.json`: an account holding a Standard seat

**G.1 Two ways, one contract.** **Path A, hosted:** the card runs every step (sign-in, the key
on-ramp, licence choice, Replace, consent, return), and an app with no UI code opens a URL or shows
a device code. That is §A–§F unchanged (`licenseChoice: "card"`). **Path B, native:** the card
authenticates (and asks consent when due), then the app's kit, or the app's own UI on the SDK's
primitives, runs the licence choice and Replace through a **pending sign-in grant**
(`licenseChoice: "app"`). Both paths use the same `LicenseChoiceView`, `ReplaceView` and
`LicenseChoiceInput`, the rules of §A, §B and §F, the same errors and `freeAccountDevice()`.
Credentials stay on the card or a platform sheet (D17).

**G.2 The declaration.** The app says where the licence is chosen when it starts the sign-in:

| Entry                                                                                 | How                                                                                                        | Default  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| `POST /<p>/identity/request` (PX-W13 §2.5; I-15's loopback, scheme and claimed HTTPS) | body member `licenseChoice`; the response echoes the effective value                                       | `"card"` |

**G.3 The card in app mode** (PX-14, I-08). After authentication and the email gate the card

**G.4 Completion.** When the flow is in app mode and a choice is due (§A.3–§A.4: a candidate, a

`POST /<p>/identity/token` answer `200 {"status": "choose", "grant": "sg_…", "expiresIn": 300,

re-authentication of a bound device, a key on-ramp choice already made) they answer the activation
response as today. Only the `choose` body has a `status` member, so a client tells the two apart by
that member alone.

**G.5 The grant routes** (I-08). Device-facing, Identity on only, `POST` with JSON, `no-store`,
`X-PKey-Device` and the metadata headers required. The grant travels in the body, never in a URL.

| Route                                | Body                                  | Answer                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

instead**: PX-17's claim with `surface: "app"`, counted as a key entry because it enrols a new

does. The other errors are `400 invalid_grant` (unknown, expired, consumed, or another device),
`409 license_choice_required`, `404 not_found`, `device_limit` with a fresh `view`, and
`429 rate_limited` with `retryAfter`. **No new error code.**

**G.6 Security** (THREAT-MODEL row by I-08).

- **The grant** is 32 random bytes, `sg_` plus base64url, stored hashed in the flow's KV record. It
  lives 300 s from issue, inside the five-minute fresh sign-in that Replace treats as its step-up
  (§B). It is bound to the product, the account, the pairwise subject and the redeeming
  `X-PKey-Device`, and, through `redirect/token`, to the PKCE verifier. `complete` or `cancel`
  consumes it, and a flow has at most one.
- **Scope.** `choice/devices` answers only for a licence in the grant's view whose `replace.allowed`
  is true, and lists only that licence's seat-holding devices. Every other id (unknown, another
  account's, not replaceable) gets the same `404 not_found`, so nothing can be enumerated. Nothing
  is ever looked up by licence key.
- **Budgets.** 30 view and device reads per grant. A Replace charges `portalDeviceDisconnect`,
  shared with the portal and the card. I-08's per-product start limits apply.
- **Replace** is `freeAccountDevice()` with every rule of §B, in one batch (§F.3). The audit row
  `portal.device.disconnect` reads "to sign in <label> in <App>", and the device-replaced email is
  sent.
- **Device code never issues a grant.** It is the cross-device channel that can be phished (R1-07,
  S-16 §5.4 item 6). A grant there would give whoever holds the code the account's licence and
  device lists and a Replace the person never sees. On the card the person sees the confirm that
  names both devices. The same-device channels (loopback, scheme, claimed HTTPS, web redirect,
  native token) are bound by PKCE or a platform token, so the grant reaches only the app in front
  of the person.
- **What the app learns:** the account's licences for this product and, for a replaceable one, its
  device labels. The person already sees these on the card, and now sees them on their own device
  after authenticating.

**G.7 Wire classification.** Additive and opt-in: one request member, one `authorize` parameter,

## Owner decisions (delegated to Claude, 2026-10-05)

decided.

1. **"Create a new license".** Offered only when the policy auto-issues to this person **and**

   - **Order:** EmailGateStep (when shown) → LicenseChoiceStep → the consent confirm (first time,

### 2.2 Key entry (§12.2; I-09)

A **key entry** is `POST /<p>/license/activate` (surface `app`), `POST
/<p>/identity/session/license` (surface `browser`) or a portal Activate License submission (surface
`portal`, I-11). **Enrolled** means a `devices` row with this `(product, device_id)`, this
`license_id` and `status = 'authorized'`. Order on the two device routes, Identity on:

1. existing rate limit, key and licence lookup (unchanged);
2. if the device is enrolled: proceed as today, never refuse, never count;
3. if `licenses.account_id IS NOT NULL` → `403 {"error":"license_owned","signInUrl":…}`, nothing
   counted (D24);
4. if the count of `license_key_entries` rows for the licence ≥ the limit →
   `403 {"error":"key_entry_limit","manageUrl":…,"keyEntries":{"used":n,"limit":n}}`;
5. `authorizeDevice` as today (`device_limit`, `fingerprint_required`, `hardware_mismatch`); on
   success insert one `license_key_entries` row and answer the activation response plus
   `"keyEntries": {"used", "limit"}`.

Refused and failed attempts never count; refresh, offline grace, documents, `license/enroll`,
store binding and sign-in-based activation never count. A licence with only a legacy `sub` and no
`account_id` is not owned (§8 Q6). Concurrent new devices at `limit − 1` may overshoot by the
number of racing requests; the limit is an on-ramp nudge, D24 is the security boundary (§8 Q5).

`manageUrl` = `<origin>/activate?product=<slug>`; `signInUrl` =
`<origin>/signin?product=<slug>` (§8 Q4). Both are built by the Worker from its own origin,
never carry the key, and are not auth failures: an SDK never wipes state or retries on either.

### 2.3 Attach, subject and sign-out (§12.3; I-09)

All take `Authorization: Bearer pkeyt_…` and `X-PKey-Device`, are `no-store`, and answer CORS for
the product's `web.origins` without credentials.

- **`POST /<p>/identity/attach`** `{ "confirm": boolean }` attaches the device's floating licence
  to the account signed in on the device (`devices.subject`), P1-07 style.
  - `confirm: false` → `200 { "status":"confirm", "license": {id, tierId, name|null} }`; no write.
  - `confirm: true` → `200` activation response (`token` rotated, `schemaVersion`, `device`,
    `license`) plus `"subject"` and `"attached": "claimed"`; already attached to this account →
    the same with no `attached`.
  - Errors: `401 unauthorized`; `403 account_required` (no sign-in on the device); `403
license_owned` (attached to another account; no `signInUrl`); `403 license_email_bound`
    (email-bound, no verified match, `claimByKey` false); `404 not_found` (no licence on the
    device, or Identity off).
- **`GET /<p>/identity/subject`** → `200 { "subject": "ps_…" | null }`.
- **`POST /<p>/identity/signout`** → `200 { "released": boolean }`. Runs Core's clearing hook for
  this device. `released: true` means the device was also deauthorized, which happens only when its
  licence belongs to the signed-out account and the device was bound by sign-in
  (`devices.bound_by = 'signin'`; §8 Q3). SDKs run Cloud Sync's flush-before-sign-out first
  (S-17 §5.4 rule 5).

### 2.4 Device-code passthrough (§12.4; I-08)

The routes are unchanged; the user-code page becomes the login card with the passthrough header,
browser-bound callback (R1-07) and explicit Continue. Poll members:

- `confirm` keeps `attachable` (it already exists, `oidc.ts:1866`; S-16 called it new).
- `ready` gains **`subject`**. `identity.name` and `identity.email` appear in `confirm` and
  `ready` only for claims the person consented to share with this product on "Continue to <App>"
  (D19; §8 Q2); otherwise `identity` is omitted.

### 2.5 Web redirect (§12.5; I-08)

- **`GET /<p>/identity/authorize`** `?redirect_uri&state&code_challenge&code_challenge_method=S256`.
  `redirect_uri`'s origin must equal a `web.origins` entry exactly and its path must be in
  `identity.redirectPaths` exactly; otherwise the card renders `400 redirect_uri_mismatch` and never
  redirects. Another method, a missing `state` (16–512 chars) or a malformed challenge → card `400
bad_request`. Success: card → Continue → `302 redirect_uri?code=…&state=…`. The code is single use
  (I-02's store), lives 60 s, is bound to `(product, redirect_uri, code_challenge)`.
- **`POST /<p>/identity/redirect/token`** (JSON) `{ code, code_verifier, redirect_uri }` with
  `X-PKey-Device` and metadata headers → `200` activation response plus `subject` (`license` is
  `null` on a licence-less device). Errors (nested): `400 invalid_grant` (unknown, used or expired
  code, verifier or `redirect_uri` mismatch), `403 not_entitled`, `429 rate_limited`. CORS for
  `web.origins` only, no credentials. No token or code ever travels in a fragment. This is S-17's
  web principal. **The path is S-16's `redirect/token`, not S-17's `web/token`** (correction); I-15
  reuses it for native redirect URIs.
