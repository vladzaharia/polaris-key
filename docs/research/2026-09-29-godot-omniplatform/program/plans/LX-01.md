## Owner decision (2026-10-05): licence choice at sign-in

The owner decided on 2026-10-05 that sign-in asks the person which licence to bind; the full text
and the delegated decisions are in [`plans/I-04.md`](I-04.md), "Owner decision (2026-10-05):
licence choice at sign-in". **Phase B still changes no wire** (`PROTOCOL_VERSION` 4,
`corpusVersion` 2).

**Effect on this plan.**

- **§2.4 orders; it does not choose.** At sign-in, `chooseAnchor` becomes
  `rankAnchorCandidates(db, p, accountId, policy, now)`. It returns the ordered candidates with
  seat state, and the first free one is the **preselected** row of the card's
  `LicenseChoiceStep`. The device binds only to the licence the person picks. `rank-first`,
  `most-free-seats` and `oldest` keep their order and the `id` tiebreak.
- **The auto-issue fallback** (step 3) runs only when there are no candidates, or when the person
  explicitly picks **Create a new free licence**. That row is offered only when the policy
  auto-issues and every candidate is full.
- **Q1 is now preselection only.** A strictly higher-rank free candidate is preselected over
  **Keep the licence this device uses**. It never moves a device by itself.
- **Re-anchoring.** `reanchor: onActivation` now means an explicit pick. **Enroll supersede on
  attach** runs only when the person picked the superseding licence.
- **Transcripts.** LX-10's `signin-anchor.json` asserts the preselection order: the rank-1
  licence is preselected, then the rank-0 licence once the rank-1 licence is full. It also asserts
  binding to the explicit pick, and a 409 `license_choice_required` without one. The final step
  changes from "neither has a free seat → `not_entitled`" to "neither has a free seat → both rows
  are full, with Replace a device".

## 0. Owner decisions encoded (binding, 2026-10-04)

- **Model OC (S-19 decision 1).** A licence is an **access contract**: tier, seats, term, offline
  window, version window, channels and keys. A **grant** is one reason someone holds
  entitlements. A device runs on exactly one **anchor** licence (`devices.license_id`). Its
  entitlements are **combined** over its contributors.
- **The account in the document's inputs (decision 2).** S-16 D9 is a rule about the document's
  _content_: no user claim, no account id and no subject in `pkey-license+jws`. S-17 D20's outcome
  stands: entitlement overrides stay on the licence.
- **Holder = the account signed in on the device (decision 4, `entitlementHolder: device`).** A
  key-entry device that nobody signed in on sees its own licence and that licence's grants only.
  `owner` is an opt-in and comes with a warning.
- **Legacy mode is byte-identical (decision 5).** `entitlementModel: legacy` reproduces today's
  document, `updatedAt` and ETag included. New products start on `combined`. Existing products stay
  on `legacy` until an operator reads the holder report and switches.
- **Decisions 3 and 6–24 as recommended.** In particular: `anchorPolicy: rank-first`; purchases are
  not cancelled by overrides in `combined` mode; the grace clamp is on by default; holder bindings
  and restore policies; `reanchor: onActivation`; "download ticket"; Cloud Sync `byEntitlement`;
  licence-less devices need an anchor for `licensed` and `entitled` gates.
- **Settings home (S-18 D2, D5 and D19).** Every licensing setting is a **claimable
  `product_settings` row** in S-18's registry: a console edit claims the value and Revert returns
  it to the manifest. There is no `products.licensing_json`. The account is not a settings scope.
- **Carried from I-04 and S-16.** D24: an owned licence's key is refused on a new device when
  Identity is on. D25: per-product removal keeps the licence. The global account id never leaves
  the Worker. I-05's `licenses.account_id`, `devices.subject` and `devices.bound_by` are used as
  merged.

## 2. Contract (server semantics; no wire text changes in phase B)

`WIRE-CONTRACT-V4.md`, `shared-protocol`, `shared-jws` and `client-core` **do not change**. One

### 2.1 Vocabulary (glossary in `start/concepts.md`, rule 4; LX-22)

**licence**, **unowned licence** (S-16's "floating"; the docs write "unowned (floating)" on first
use), **grant**, **grant holder** (exactly one of: account, licence or store identity),
**entitlement** with its kind (`feature`, `ownership`, `policy`, or the reserved `quota`),
**anchor**, **contributors**, **combine rule** and **effective set**. All as defined in S-19 §7.1.
"Store grant" becomes "a grant with a store source". PX-W3's `?grant=` becomes `?ticket=`.

### 2.2 Contributors (S-19 §7.3.1, as accepted)

For device `d` of product `p` at time `now`:

- **A (anchor)** is `licenses` row `(p, d.license_id)`. When `license_id = ''` (`NO_LICENSE_ID`)
  the device has no anchor.
- **H (holder account)** depends on `entitlementHolder`. Under `device`, H is the account whose
  pairwise subject is `d.subject` (`resolveSubject`, following aliases), or none. Under `owner`, it
  is the same, but when `d.subject` is null it falls back to `A.account_id`. Products with Identity
  off never set `d.subject`, so under `device` their grants are created **licence-held** (S-19
  §7.3.1).
- **S (store identities)** are the `device_store_identities` rows of `(p, d)` with
  `verified_at ≥ now − 7 days` (Steam).
- **Licence-less devices** have no licence document. They keep today's answer (§4 "The licence-less device today"). Cloud Sync
  `requiresFlag` and the issuer's claims read H's and S's grants through the same rules
  (decision 23).
- **The portal and developer backends** call `resolveSubjectEntitlements(db, p, subject, now)`.
  This is the same function with a synthetic device: no anchor, H is the subject's account, S is
  empty, and every one of H's usable licences contributes.

### 2.3 Combination (S-19 §7.3.2 and §7.4, as accepted)

**`legacy`.** Only A is read. The layer stack is: tier profile → licence profiles → **store
layer** → **oidc layer** → licence overrides → device overrides → `injectAdminPolicy(A)`.

- The **store layer** holds A's licence-held `grants` with a store source (`app-store`, `play`,
  `steam`) and `state = 'active'`. Each key renders as `{state:"default", value:true, updatedAt:
MAX(grant_entitlements.updated_at)}`. That is exactly today's `storeGrantLayer`
  (`core/storeGrants.ts`), which takes `MAX(granted_at)` per flag.
- The **oidc layer** holds A's `source = 'oidc'` grant, with each entry's stored `state`, `value`
  and `updated_at`.
- Keys never overlap between the oidc layer and the licence overrides: LX-08 step 4 removes them
  from `overrides_json`. Splitting one layer into two disjoint layers therefore leaves
  `mergeMap`'s result unchanged.
- Nothing else is read.

**`combined`**, in this order:

1. **Licences** are `{A}` plus H's usable licences of `p` (`kind = 'base'`, or `addon` while a
   contributing base is usable).
2. **Slices.** Each licence gets tier profile → licence profiles → licence entitlement overrides.
   A's slice also gets A's device overrides for **reserved keys**, then `injectAdminPolicy(A)`.
   Every other licence's slice gets `injectAdminPolicy(L)`.
3. **Grants.** The licence-held grants of every contributing licence, H's account-held grants and
   S's store-held grants count when all of these hold:
   - `state` is `active` or `past_due`, or it is `refunded`/`revoked` with `grace_until > now`;
   - `expires_at` is null or later than `now`;
   - `state` is not `suppressed`.
4. **Combine** each key by its rule (table below).
   - A `feature` or `ownership` key is never cancelled by a slice.
   - A `policy` key takes A's value when A's slice is `enforced` or `hidden`.
   - The entry's state is the strongest state among the contributors equal to the result.
   - `updatedAt` is the maximum over **all** contributors, including those that lost.
5. **Non-reserved device overrides** apply last, with `mergeMap`.
6. **Terms come from A only:** `expires_at`, seats, and `max_offline_days`. The offline days fall
   back to the tier's `policy_offline_grace_days`, then to the product default. Config is never
   combined.

| Key                                 | Rule                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `channels`                          | union                                                                                                       |
| `app.minVersion`, `app.maxVersion`  | widest window (lowest min, highest max)                                                                     |
| `deviceLimit`                       | **A only**: the tier default, else the licence value, plus the sum of seat-pack grants on A                 |
| `license.tier`, `license.tierLabel` | the highest `tiers.rank` among contributing licences; ties go to A                                          |
| `license.*`, `app.*`, `pkey.*`      | reserved prefixes; rule 9 warning, then error (LX-05, LX-05b)                                               |
| any other flag                      | catalog `combine`, else by JSON type: boolean `any`, number `max`, string array `union`, otherwise `anchor` |

### 2.4 Anchor rule (LX-10; I-09 ships steps 1–3 inline until then)

`chooseAnchor(db, p, accountId, policy, now)`:

1. **Candidates** are licences with `account_id = H`, `kind = 'base'`, `superseded_by IS NULL`
   and `licenseUsable`, where the count of `authorized` devices is below the effective
   `deviceLimit`.
2. **Order** depends on the policy:
   - `rank-first`: `tiers.rank DESC, (expires_at IS NULL) DESC, expires_at DESC, activated_at
ASC, id ASC`. The final `id` key is new; it makes the choice deterministic.
   - `most-free-seats`: free seats DESC, then the `rank-first` keys.
   - `oldest`: `activated_at ASC, id ASC`.
3. **No candidate:** auto-issue when `autoIssue` allows it (origin `oidc`, as in
   `activateFromIdentity`). Otherwise answer today's `not_entitled`, with no new member.

A device that already holds a usable anchor follows §8 Q1. Re-anchoring happens only at an
explicit activation (`reanchor: onActivation`). It is audited and seat-checked. Key entry keeps
binding to the entered licence (D24 unchanged).

### 2.5 Lifecycle (LX-12)

A licence's `status` stays `active`/`disabled`. `ended_reason` (`revoked`, `refunded`,
`chargeback`, `superseded`) is written in the same statement that disables it. `superseded_by` may
be set while the licence is still active (an enrolled free licence that has been attached). Grant
`state` moves as follows:

- `active` → `refunded`, `revoked` or `suppressed`;
- `active` → `past_due` → `active` or `revoked`;
- a refund sets `grace_until = now + refundGraceHours` (0 means immediate), and a chargeback never
  gets grace.

Every write to a licence, grant, override or device bumps the version of the holder it touches, in
the same D1 batch.

## 8. Risks and open questions

### Open questions for the owner

1. **Sign-in on a device that already has a usable anchor.** **Recommend:** keep the anchor, unless
   H holds a candidate with a strictly higher `tiers.rank`; then move the device to it,
   seat-checked. A `legacy` product would otherwise hide a person's better licence on that device.
2. **Default `entitlementModel`.** A registry default is a single value, and a migration-written row
   would fight the manifest. **Recommend** a derived default: `legacy` when `products.created_at`
   is before a constant committed in LX-06, else `combined`. A manifest value or a console claim
   overrides it.
3. **`refundGraceHours` against S-18's NOT_A_SETTING "refund always revokes".** **Recommend:** keep
   the setting, capped at 168 hours. It only delays the revocation and never prevents it; S-18's
   A.4 row is reworded to say so.
4. **`entitlements.changed` delivery.** `subject_events` has `CHECK (type IN ('subject.merged',
'subject.deleted'))`, which cannot widen without a rebuild, and an unowned licence has no
   subject. **Recommend:** a Core table `entitlement_events` with a pull cursor now, and webhooks
   later, as in I-04 Q8. LX-13 drops "webhook" from its scope.
5. **Where `restorePolicy` and `transferCooldownDays` live.** **Recommend:** per store, inside the
   existing commerce connector settings (`dist_connector_settings`, `commerce/settings.ts`), which
   are operator-only and already per store. That replaces the separate `dist_commerce_settings`
   table. LX-11 confirms this in its plan.
6. **The licence-less 401.** **Recommend:** in LX-18, add `reason: "no_license"` to that 401, so new
   SDKs show `needs-activation` instead of `revoked`. Phase B leaves it as it is.
7. **Grace clamp contract text.** **Recommend:** LX-07 adds one informative §3.6 sentence now
   ("`graceUntil` may be earlier when the licence expires sooner"). Verifiers are unaffected.
8. **`addon` licences.** **Recommend:** add the column now, but let nothing write `addon` until
   LX-25's plan defines what entering such a key does.

### Risks

- Byte identity depends on copying `updatedAt` exactly; LX-09's live snapshot is the gate.
- The per-request cost of the resolver is measured in LX-09.
- Settings depend on ST-01b and ST-03 landing first. Without them LX-06 blocks; LX-08 does not.
- The plan is longer than two pages because it is the shared plan of six packages. It is not split
  because each package executes disjoint sections.
