## Goal

Every Edition 04 brand asset is generated by `pnpm gen:brand`, checked by `--check`, and rendered in `dist/preview`: the ten service icons with a sprite, `serviceIconSvg()` and `<ServiceIcon>`; Delivery-named marks, web manifest and social images; 1080x1350 portrait cards; the trimmed horizontal lockup for the 64 px console header; a generated-asset hash manifest; the service icon tile spec; and the commerce and packs glyph rules.

## Why

The new brand guide asks for assets the repo does not generate yet. Console and docs work (ST-48, ST-50, DOC-02a/b) consume them, so they come from one generator, not hand-placed files.
