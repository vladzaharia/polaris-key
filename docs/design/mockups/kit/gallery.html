Turn off Licensing?

Commerce needs Licensing, so it turns off too, in the same change:

  • Commerce: Diceroll’s 4 offers stop selling on App Store, Google Play and Steam.

Licenses and purchases are kept. Turning Licensing back on doesn’t turn Commerce on.

Delete Diceroll?

Every license and device stops working, and the slug stays reserved. This can’t be undone.
