Turn off Licensing for Diceroll?

Commerce needs Licensing, so it turns off too, in the same change.

LicensingOnOff
Players already activated keep playing until their offline window ends, up to 30 days. After that, and on every new install, Diceroll can’t confirm a license, Free tier included. 11,239 devices checked a license in the last 30 days.
CommerceNeeds LicensingOnOff
The game can’t show Diceroll’s 4 offers or confirm a purchase, so buying packs in the game stops and restores are refused.

Licenses, tiers, offers and purchases are kept.

Turning Licensing back on with its switch leaves Commerce off; reverting this change in Activity turns both back on.
