Sidebar contexts

The console’s sidebar shows one context at a time, switched from its header. Platform pages never appear inside a product, and a product’s pages never appear on the Platform.

One context at a timeOpening a product switches to its context; Platform in the switcher switches back to Home.
Features drive the groupsTurning a feature on adds its group in canonical order; turning it off removes the group and its pages.
Hidden, never dead-endedA page someone can’t use isn’t in their sidebar. A link to it from elsewhere opens a page that names who can give access.
