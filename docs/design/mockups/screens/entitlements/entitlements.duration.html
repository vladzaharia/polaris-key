What a Pro license doesBought Oct 7, 2026

Oct 7, 2026 Starts Every DJDL release on the stable and beta tracks, with lossless audio, stem separation and 500 synced crates.
Oct 7, 2027 Renews for another year When its subscription renews. Nothing changes on the holder’s Macs and PCs.
If a renewal doesn’t arrive Past due for 7 days Still works, with every update, while the holder sorts out payment.
7 days later Keeps the last version Runs the newest stable release from before the lapse (4.2.0 if it lapsed today), on the stable track, for good. The portal says “Updates ended at 4.2.0 · keeps working”.
Any time after Renewing brings updates back The same license gets every release again. Nothing to reactivate.
