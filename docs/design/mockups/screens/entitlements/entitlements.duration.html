What a Pro license does

  1. Oct 7, 2026 Starts Every release on the stable and beta tracks, with everything Pro includes.
  2. Oct 7, 2027 Renews for another year Nothing changes on the holder’s devices.
  3. If a renewal doesn’t arrive Past due for 7 days Grace: still works, with every update.
  4. 7 days later Keeps the last version 4.2.0 if it lapsed today.
