Devices

Move your license to a new computer

Free a device, then activate the app on the new computer.

Important
Removing a device signs the app out there. The app’s developer sets the device limit.

Use the old computer

  1. 1

    Open your library

    Sign in at key.plrs.im and select DJDL.

  2. 2

    Free the old device

    Select Free a device, choose the old computer and select Remove Booth PC.

  3. 3

    Activate the new computer

    Open DJDL and sign in or enter your license key.

If the old computer is gone

Use the same library steps. Pick the device by its name and last-seen date.

Check it worked

The new computer appears under Devices.

Undo

Sign in to DJDL again on the old computer if there is a free device slot.

Still need help?

For refunds or app problems, ask the app’s developer.
