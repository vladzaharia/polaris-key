Help a customer

Fix a lost key, changed hardware or a device limit from the console.

Role required: Product adminLicensing
Impact
Deauthorizing a device signs it out of DJDL. The license stays active.

Free a device for a customer

  1. 1

    Find the license

    Open Licenses Search for kenji@example.com and open the DJDL license.

  2. 2

    Deauthorize the old device

    Open Devices, select Booth PC, then select Deauthorize Booth PC.

  3. 3

    Confirm the customer can continue

    The device count drops. Ask the customer to activate DJDL on the new computer.

What the customer sees

Move your license to a new computerThe public Help article for the customer.
