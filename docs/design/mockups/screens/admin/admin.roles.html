Members

Four built-in roles. A person can hold several, and what they can do is everything their roles allow, added together.

Members 10 SSO rules 3 Roles

Superadmin

Everything, in every product, including products added later. The only role that edits SSO rules.

Given by a Superadmin or the root rule2 people

Platform admin

Platform settings, connections, packages and status, and new products. Sees other products by name only.

Given by a Superadmin1 person

Product admin

Everything in one product, or in all products. Can be narrowed to some areas, such as Ship builds and Commerce.

Given by a Superadmin or that product’s admin5 people

Console access

Can sign in and read the docs. Every other role includes it, so it’s only shown on its own.

Given by a Superadmin or a Platform admin1 person
