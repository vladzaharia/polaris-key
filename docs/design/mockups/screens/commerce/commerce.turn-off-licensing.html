Turn off Licensing for Diceroll?

New players can’t activate Diceroll. Players already activated keep playing until their 14 offline days run out.

Commerce needs Licensing, so it turns off too, in the same change:

  • Commerce: the App Store, Google Play and Steam keep selling Diceroll’s 4 offers, but new purchases won’t reach players’ licenses.
Take the offers off sale first
Stop selling on an offer takes it off the App Store and Google Play. Steam has no API for this, so change the game and its DLC in Steamworks.
Open Offers

18,240 licenses, their add-ons and every past purchase are kept. Turning Licensing back on doesn’t turn Commerce back on.
