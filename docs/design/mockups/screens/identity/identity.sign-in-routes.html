After Continue: where the address goes

DJDL’s sign-in card, one step after identity.sign-in, for four addresses and one saved passkey. The header and footer stay put; only the body changes (SIGN-IN.md §4.16). Lakeside University is a platform connection with three domains (identity.connection).

How the card picks the way in

One check on the typed address, server-side.

  1. The address’s domain is a verified domain of a connection: the card offers that connection’s sign-in first. Platform connections route on every product’s card; a product connection routes only on its own product’s card.
  2. That domain has SSO required: the connection is the only way in. No email code, no passkey, and no fallback while the connection is down. A saved passkey picked on the first step is refused for such an address and routed to the connection instead (pin 5).
  3. Any other address gets an email code. A saved passkey was already offered on the first step, and a remembered method shows as “You usually sign in with …”.
  4. The answer reveals only that a domain uses single sign-on, never whether an account exists. The check is rate-limited.
  5. Domains are re-checked daily. A domain whose DNS record disappears stops routing at once.
