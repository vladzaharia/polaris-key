After Continue: where the address goes

Only the body changes. In a landscape window the passport stays beside it (the pins are the form pane), on a phone the header strip stays above it.

How the card picks the way in

  1. A verified domain of a connection: the card offers that connection’s sign-in first. Platform connections route on every product’s card, a product connection only on its own.
  2. SSO required: the connection is the only way in, with no email code, passkey or fallback while it’s down. A saved passkey for such an address is refused and routed there (pin 5).
  3. Any other address gets an email code. A remembered method shows as “You usually sign in with …”.
  4. The answer reveals only that a domain uses single sign-on, never whether an account exists. The check is rate-limited.
  5. Domains are re-checked daily; one whose DNS record disappears stops routing at once.
