Turn off Sign-in for Diceroll?

Cloud Sync needs Sign-in, so it turns off too, in the same change.

Sign-inOnOff
Nobody can sign in to Diceroll: not on its sign-in card, Game Center, Play Games or Steam. Players signed in now are signed out the next time the game checks in, within an hour. The Diceroll accounts connection stops exchanging Firebase tokens. Activated devices keep playing; new devices activate with a license key.
Cloud SyncNeeds Sign-inOnOff
3,412 players’ saves and dice sets stop syncing. Each device keeps what it has.

Accounts, their links to Diceroll accounts, consents and synced saves are kept. Licenses stay in players’ libraries.

Turning Sign-in back on with its switch leaves Cloud Sync off; reverting this change in Activity turns both back on.
