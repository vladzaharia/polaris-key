## 2. What Polaris Key is, and what it isn't

**We are** the service behind the scenes that:

- checks whether your licence is valid and how many devices can use it;
- delivers app downloads and updates;
- runs your Polaris Key account, so one sign-in works across apps.

**We are not** the maker or seller of the apps. Each app is made and sold by its **developer**.
That means:

- **Your licence is a deal between you and the developer.** What the app does, what you paid,
  what the licence includes, refunds and app support are all the developer's responsibility.
- **The developer sets the rules of the licence**: how many devices, when it expires, which
  versions it covers, and whether it works offline and for how long.
- **The developer can change or cancel a licence.** For example, after a refund. We carry out what
  they decide.
- **An app's own terms apply too.** Some apps ask you to accept their terms when you sign in. We
  record that you accepted, but those terms are between you and the developer.

- Your account is free.

- **Keep your sign-in safe.** Anyone who can read your email or use your Google, Apple or Steam
  account can sign in as you. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has got in.
- Use your real email address, and one you can receive mail at. We send sign-in codes and security
  notices there.
- One person per account. Don't share an account or sign in as someone else.
- You must be at least [[OWNER: minimum age]] to have an account.
- You can delete your account at any time in the portal: **Account → Your data → Delete my
  account**. It's immediate and can't be undone. Your licences stay with the developers who sold

## 4. Licence keys and devices

- **A licence key is like a password.** Keep it private. Anyone who has your key can try to use
  your licence. We keep only a scrambled copy, so **we can't show you a lost key again**. See the
  Help page for what to do.
- Each licence has a device limit set by the developer. You can free a device yourself in the
  portal or in the app.
- A device that hasn't checked in for 90 days loses its seat automatically, so the seat can be used
  elsewhere. If it comes back and a seat is free, it takes one again.
- Apps check your licence with our servers from time to time. If an app can't reach us for longer
  than the developer allows, it may ask you to go online briefly.

- try to break into, overload or disrupt our servers, or probe them for weaknesses without our
  permission (if you find a security problem, please tell us: [[OWNER: security contact]]);

- use the service to break the law or someone else's rights.

If you do, we can block the activity, suspend or close your account, and tell the affected
developer. Where it's reasonable, we'll warn you first and tell you why.
